# Handoff: wave GT3, guided templates: conditions (15 November 2026)

## State

- **Built:** spec 074, wave GT3. Report: `docs/reports/2026-11-15-wave-gt3.md`. Decisions TPL-1463 to TPL-1476 are in the spec's decision table, each "decided on the product owner's behalf on 2026-11-15, may be overruled".
- **No migration.** Conditions live in the manifest of a version (on entries, steps, the settings of questions and `label_rules`) and in the texts (`conditional` blocks), so no number is taken.
- **Experimental:** everything of GT3 is part of `guided_templates`, off by default. The three new routes answer `experimental_off` while it is off; a template with any condition is guided, so it is not offered while the part is off.

## For whoever lands this

- **Base:** main `610c27c` (PB3 and DC4). GT3 touches `internal/pages/sets.go` (`SetPage.SkipFiles`), `internal/pages/kinds.go` (a `conditional` block is refused outside templates), `internal/attachments/attachments.go` (`CopyAllExcept`, which `CopyAll` now calls) and `internal/document` (the block `conditional`). No migration and no shared table.
- **DC5:** `internal/conditions` exists now. `Combine` and `MaxTests` are the shared part the study names (TPL-433); the subjects and operators of transitions belong beside them, with their own table of cases.
- **The editor:** `EditorActions` has `openSection`; `DocumentEditor` takes `templateConditions`; the block menu offers "Change the condition" for a section. `SubjectPicker` takes `groupsOnly`.
- **The order of a form** is defined twice, in `formOrder` (`internal/templates/rules.go`) and in `stepsOf` (`web/src/components/template-form.tsx`): step by step, then the questions of no step. A change to one must change the other, or the browser's preview and the server's decision part ways.
- **Pictures:** `template-settings-form`, `template-pages`, `template-field-dialog` and `template-versions` changed (the Condition column, Rename, the Rules section in the sidebar); seven are new. A wave that changes the template settings' sidebar will change them again.
