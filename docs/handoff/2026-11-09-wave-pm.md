# Handoff: wave PM (spec 045)

- `post.create` and `database.create`, split off `page.create` with migration 0041; nobody gains or loses anything (proof in spec 045 and the report).
- Every way to make an item asks for the permission of its kind through `permissions.CreatePermission`.
- Screens offer only what a person may make. Bundles are format 4.
- PRM-206, PRM-211 (export stays one permission) and PRM-215 are built.

## Watch out

- Migration numbers must be contiguous: this wave took 0041. A wave that lands later renumbers its own.
- Docker restarted by itself twice during this wave's browser runs and stopped every container; start the wave's containers again with `scripts/dev up` before a gate.
