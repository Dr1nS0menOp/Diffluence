# Handoff: wave SC5, the settings of a space (spec 083)

**Done.** The settings of a space are rebuilt as blueprint 03 arranges them (ADR-050), steps 0 to 6 and 9 of spec 083:

- **Sidebar:** a head that stays in place (Back to the space, **Space settings**, the badge and name, the search field), four entries with icons (Automation while on, People, Content, Details), a divider, and five groups that fold, General, Access, Pages and files, Look and Connections, stored per person under `space-…` ids. The search field finds the names of sections an entry took over, Enter opens the first match, Escape empties it. An entry opened by address is scrolled into view.
- **Registry:** `web/src/views/space-sections.ts` is the one list of entries: place, width, the permissions that open them, the address of a screen of its own, aliases, old names, document. Its unit test holds what opened each part before this wave and proves that no move narrowed it. The space menu derives Space settings from it.
- **Screens of their own:** Content, Trash, Automation, Webhooks, Templates, Insights, Public site, Permissions and Audit keep their addresses and open beside the settings sidebar through `SpaceToolFrame` for whoever may open space settings; everyone else sees them in the space as before.
- **Sections:** Details (one form, ordered save, `space-details-save.ts`), Picture of the space, Statuses and approvals, Layout (tiles), Archive the space, Delete the space, Pages still to write (a table), Files and Archived pages, Features (a card of switches); the rest moved unchanged under `SettingsSection`.
- **Components:** `Switch`, `ChoiceTiles`, `useSaved` (toast); everything else is SC4's.
- **API:** spec 083, "Through the API", lists for every entry the routes that read and change it. No route was missing, none was added.

No migration and no server change. Report: `docs/reports/2026-11-20-wave-sc5.md`.

**Not built, by decision:**

- Step 7, People's row of tools (search, Kind, Role, Add people, Export as CSV in the title row, the filter to administrators), goes to the wave of the tool rows with ADM-103 (D33). People is today's role list in its new place.
- Step 8, the column order, actions last and sort marks of the tables, and the empty block for Automation, Templates and Document control (SC4 D24), go to that same wave (D34).
- Screens of their own keep their own heading until their own wave (D21).

**For wave UR1 (spec 094).** Its entry for what someone without access sees goes in the Access group after Permissions. The registry has a comment at that place. Whichever of UR1 and SC5 lands second adds one line to `SPACE_ENTRIES` (with `place: 'space-access'`, its needs and width), the words `spaceSections.<id>.name` and `.intro` in `en.json`, and one case in `SectionContent` of `views/space-settings.tsx` (or a `path` if it is a screen of its own); the registry's unit test lists the order of the Access group and must be updated with it.

**For the product owner to decide.** D20 to D34 in the spec are decided on your behalf and may be overruled. The ones that matter most:

- **D20:** someone who only manages access sees no Space settings item in the space menu, as before; their People item opens People beside the settings sidebar.
- **D24:** the last owner cannot be removed in the form, but the server still accepts an empty list of owners. Refusing it on the server is a small API change for a later wave.
- **D33 and D34:** the row of tools of People and the order of the tables wait for the tool rows wave.
