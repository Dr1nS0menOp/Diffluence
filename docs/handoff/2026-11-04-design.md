# Handoff 2026-11-04: the design and its standard

## What was done

- The product owner chose after ten designs and two iterations: **Helder** for style and layout, the **Rail** editor, and a page tree with **collapsible branches**. ADR-021 records it.
- `docs/design/README.md` is the standard. `CLAUDE.md` makes it a hard rule, and the pull request template and the definition of done refer to it.
- All values live in `web/src/design/tokens.css`. `web/scripts/check-design.mjs` runs in `make check`, and `check-design.test.mjs` proves each rule fails on a violation.
- Every `…View` has an approved picture in `web/e2e/design-baselines/`, also dark, narrow and right-to-left. A new screen without a picture fails the check.
- The whole frontend was rebuilt on this. `window.confirm` and `window.prompt` are gone. No new dependency.
- The system font is the default, and a font can be imported in the settings (spec 006). New routes `GET /api/v1/appearance`, `GET /api/v1/appearance/font` (both public, justified in the code) and `PUT`, `DELETE /api/v1/admin/appearance/font`. Event `settings.font_changed`.
- The byline needs names, so `pages.Detail` now has `created_by_name` and `updated_by_name`.
- Also in this round: the `create-admin` race with a migrating server, and the lock in `db.GrantApp`.

## Decisions taken while translating the design

- Rail kept global navigation in the editor. In Helder that means the top bar stays and the sidebar goes.
- The welcome uses the full display name. Taking the first word is wrong for many names.
- Tree rows only have the add button. The design also showed a More button per row. Its actions are in the page's own More menu, so that was left out for now.
- The two levels nearest the top start open, because the product owner wants to see the branching and then collapse it.

## Unfinished, or seen along the way

- Comparing versions glues list items together ("buddySet up your laptop"): the plain text of a list has no separator. An older bug in the comparison, not in the design. Not fixed here.
- `web/src/views/admin.tsx` holds all seven tabs in one file. It was only touched where needed.
- The pictures cover the states they show. Screens in other states, such as an admin tab other than Settings, are covered by the rules, not by a picture.
- `.github/workflows/ci.yml` runs the pictures in the same Playwright image as local runs. Updating Playwright means regenerating them.
