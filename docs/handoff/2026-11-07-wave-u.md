## What is built

- The lock beside a page title is open or closed (ACC-066).
- The spaces in the administration filter by name, pages, storage and quiet months (ADM-033).
- The Content screen filters by the day of the last change and of creation (SPC-061).
- The user list names the spaces of each guest (ACC-040).
- A person's screen in the administration shows groups, spaces and how many pages they own, without titles (ADM-010, partly).
- The attention list warns when the disk for files is nearly full (ADM-049, partly).
- EDT-150 is measured: about 10 ms a key on a page of 100,000 words.
