# UI test, 6 November 2026

A walk through the real screens, in a real browser, with the example space on. The regression
tests are in `web/e2e/walk.spec.ts`, plus one in `web/e2e/blocks.spec.ts`. The throwaway scripts and
test accounts are not in the repository.

## What was tested

- **Top bar and sidebar:** every menu, the space strip, the tree, filter, Create and More to create.
- **Pages:** all 21 entries of the page menu, except Delete and Archive one by one; Delete was
  walked apart. History, compare, labels, reactions, favourites, watching, Present, Reader mode,
  Print, Export, public link, Turn into folder, Copy, Move, Rename, Approvals, Mark as verified.
- **Editor:** all 60 entries of the `/` menu, the table menu, undo and redo, leaving a code block,
  reload and back in the middle of typing, publish, two people on one page without the collaboration
  service.
- **Together:** comments, mentions, replies, notifications, tasks given to a person.
- **Spaces:** Overview, People, Blog, Files, Archive, Settings, Access, Content, Trash, public sites.
- **Other:** all 22 administration screens, the account tabs, People, profile, notification settings,
  Watching, Tasks, search, the 18 pages of the example space, databases, the PDF viewer, templates,
  Markdown export, a Markdown import up to its preview, a new public site.
- **People:** administrator, member, guest and stranger, by menu and by direct address.
- **Looks:** dark, 390 pixels wide, right to left, Tab order and focus rings.
- **Wrong use:** empty fields, long titles, unbroken titles, odd labels, double clicks, back,
  reload, wrong addresses, deleted pages.

Not confirmed by this walk: drag and paste. My scripts did not get a block dragged or a paste in, so
these rest on the existing tests in `web/e2e/blocks.spec.ts` and `web/e2e/writing.spec.ts`. Also not
walked: a public link opened by a stranger (it needs the public host), publishing a public site to
its end, the collaboration service and a real phone.

## What held up

No script errors and no server errors in any walk. Restricted pages, private spaces and the
administration gave nothing away to a member, guest or stranger, and asked for a login first where
that fits. After login, a deep address comes back. Two people publishing get "Someone else
published", and nothing is lost. Text typed just before a reload or back comes back through "This
browser kept text that was not saved". Dark and right to left look finished.

## Found and fixed

Worst first.

| What | Fix |
| --- | --- |
| The PDF and spreadsheet viewer was small | It fills the window, with a named Close button; Escape also works |
| A double click on Create made two pages | One at a time, in all five create dialogs |
| A double click on Post made two comments | The comment box sends once |
| A long title without spaces pushed the page 3355 pixels wide, also in Content | Titles and table links wrap |
| New space hid its Create button below the fold | Buttons of a tall dialog stay in view |
| The page menu said "Watch this page" to someone already watching | It asks again each time it opens |
| A colour in Space settings read `editor.color_blue` | It has its name |
| `/admin/nosuch` showed an empty Administration page | It says the page does not exist |
| A guest was told "No space fits this filter" with no filter set | It says there are no spaces yet |
| On a phone, words in tables broke per letter | Words stay whole, and the table scrolls |
| The Also editing note pushed Publish to a second line | The note wraps alone |
| Buttons in Mail and Settings stretched across the screen | They keep the size of their words |
| Groups and Provisioning showed a bare table head when empty | They say so |
| The page menu stopped at 70% of the window | It uses the height of the window |
| "Page comments" wrapped in the panel tabs | Tabs stay on one line |
| "An empty page" touched the sidebar | It has its margin |
| Two placeholders were cut off | Shorter words |

## Left

- **The Care report** gives every row of Largest files the same name, because ADM-055 reports by
  size only. React gets duplicate keys there. It needs a stable row key, and no test could show it.
- **The title field in the editor** shows one line, so a long title is cut off while writing.
- **"Give this page a title"** is cut off at phone width.
- **PdfCraft on a phone:** its own toolbar overlaps. That is the vendored viewer, which also shows
  its own Discord link.
- **The public link dialog** shows only a path, with no full address and no copy button.
- **Raw values:** `active`, `member` and `local` in lower case in the administration, permission
  keys such as `create_personal_space`, webhook event names, ids in the audit log.
- **Organising** asks for the id of a page as company home page, where a page picker would fit.
- **The history** offers "Choose two versions" when there is only one.
- **The date in the restore note** reads "from now" right after a reload.
- **The import preview** says "1 pages".

## For the product owner

Matters of taste, and all of them change the look or the structure.

- **The top bar on a phone** takes a quarter of the screen. A menu button would give that back.
- **The page menu** has 21 entries and does not fit a window of 900 pixels. Group or split it?
- **The administration** has 22 flat entries, and Features appears under both Organising and Rules.
  Appearance under Settings sits apart from Look.
- **Space settings** is one very long page.
- **The space name** shows three times: strip, sidebar head and page title.
- **Words:** "Why can or can't they?" in Page restrictions, "(Censored title)" in the audit log,
  "No days have closed yet" in Insights.
- **"What changed since your last visit"** shows to someone who just published.
- **Present** does not show the title on its first slide.
- **Rename and Save as template** have no Cancel button; other dialogs do.
- **A long title in the tree** takes many lines. Cut it at two?
