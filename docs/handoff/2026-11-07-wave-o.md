## Left

- **The public link has no full address.** The app does not know the address of the public site: an operator serves it elsewhere. The dialog shows the path, a copy button and a sentence. A setting for that address would close it.
- **Webhook event names and ids in the audit log** stay as they are. They are for people who connect systems, and ids are how the audit log works (ADR-029).
- **Page fields in block settings** still take a pasted address. `PagePicker` now exists and could replace that.
- **BLK-066, BLK-067, BLK-069** as the spec says.

## Not proven

- Rotating cards were tested with reduced motion and by hand, not by waiting for a turn.
- The timeline shows dates at their real distance, with no scale drawn. Long labels can run past a bar.
- `field-sizing` for the title works in Chromium, the only browser in the tests. Other browsers without it show one line, as before.
- Popular pages was tested with counts written straight to the table.
