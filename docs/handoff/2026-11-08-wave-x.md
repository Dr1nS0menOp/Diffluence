# Handoff 2026-11-08: wave X

Branch `wave-x`. See spec 033 for every ask and decision, and `docs/reports/2026-11-08-wave-x.md` for the report.

## What is built

- The Stil look of ADR-034: fewer lines and boxes, a quieter table, labels without an outline; `docs/design/README.md` marks what ADR-034 supersedes.
- A sidebar on the personal screens, and sidebar parts that fold and resize, kept per person.
- The tree without guide lines or its own heading, folded from the first level, with its tools as icons above it.
- Space settings and the administration in a settings frame with groups and "Jump to a setting".
- People of a space rebuilt with tabs, pictures, roles and how people got access; the user list filters by role and space.
- Automation of the instance for every space or chosen spaces.
- A search box that searches every space, with a filter panel built on the existing search syntax, and an organisation name in its placeholder.
