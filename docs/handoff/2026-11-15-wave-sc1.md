# Handoff: wave SC1, the frame (spec 078)

**Done.** The frame is rebuilt as blueprint 03 arranges it, with the product owner's confirmation of 2026-11-15 (ADR-046):

- **Top bar:** the sidebar button and the name; search with Create; the bell, help, a settings button to the administration (administrators only) and the account. The account menu has the identity block, Profile, Your account, a Theme sub-menu, a group under the instance name, and Log out.
- **Sidebar outside a space:** the global part (Home, Recent, Favourites, Spaces, Drafts, Tasks, People, Watching), which a person orders and hides for themselves, stored as the preferences `sidebar_order` and `sidebar_hidden`; panels beside the sidebar for Recent, Favourites and Spaces; the favourite spaces on the personal screens. Drafts is a new screen, `/drafts`.
- **Sidebar of a space:** the strip of spaces and the fixed Home row are gone. A header row leads home and holds the space menu (tools, Export, Automation, Space settings, Archive and Delete). The groups are Shortcuts (added in a dialog from the sidebar), Content (add, order and view, and the filter always shown), Blogs (the five newest posts), Space apps (release) and Back to top.
- **Start screen:** cards of what the person last touched, the welcome message, and the feed with pills and cards. There is no greeting, grid of spaces or side column.
- **Current item:** rounded corners, normal weight and a bar on the start edge.

No migration. Report with the before and after pictures: `docs/reports/2026-11-15-wave-sc1.md`.

**Gates, after the rebase onto GT5 and SR1:** the targeted mutations, the `space-menu` retake, `0-design.spec.ts` twice without updates and the full e2e suite pass. `make check` passes except `internal/permissions`, whose matrix test reached the 45-minute limit on the shared VM, a known VM-only timeout that passes in GitHub CI. This wave changes no code there. Three faults in the tests that the full runs found are fixed. Details are in the report.

**Unfinished, by line:** NAV-107/109 (owner, reaction button, recent-actions control, visited colour), NAV-108 (a comment in the feed needs the feed to carry it), NAV-125 (Apps, Templates), SPC-160 (drawings, document control, choosing entries), SPC-097 (Calendars).
