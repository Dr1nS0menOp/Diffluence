# Handoff: wave SC2, the page (spec 079)

**Done.** The page is rebuilt as blueprint 03 now describes it (ADR-045): a header row whose end stands outside the reading column, with the publishing state, who else is reading, Edit, Share with the lock on it joined to Copy link, and More; a byline without date or version that offers a reaction and names the state of a controlled page; More in groups with sub-menus (Watch, More details, Convert, Export, Run a rule, Archive or delete) and nothing in red; Share with one field, a General access row and its actions at the foot; and the panel of the bell with For me and Watched, a heading per day, the kind of content and the start of a comment. The full screen of notifications has the same tabs. ADR-047 records what this supersedes. Report: `docs/reports/2026-11-15-wave-sc2.md`. No migration.

**Server.** `GET /api/v1/me/notifications` takes `tab` (`for_me`, `watched`) and `excerpts`; `POST /api/v1/me/notifications/read-all` takes `tab`; items carry `page_kind`. The excerpt is read for all items in one query, by the rule of the mail excerpt. Five new mutations in `scripts/mutation-check.sh` guard the excerpt and the tabs.

**New in the components.** `MenuSub` in `components/menu.tsx` (sub-menus, placed by `submenuPlace`, which keeps them on a 360-pixel screen and mirrors in right-to-left); `.switch`; `Dialog` takes `tools` and `nearTop`; `components/restriction-state.ts` is the one place that turns the three states of ACC-064 into calls, used by the restrictions dialog and Share; `pageMenu` in `web/e2e/helpers.ts` follows sub-menus in tests.

**Not built, on purpose.** COL-104 (suggested people) waits for its own study; COL-119 and COL-120 (replying and reacting from the panel, folding updates) are a wave of their own; PAG-147 (page tools) belongs to another area, so Comments stays in More until it lands. Partly: PAG-145 (saying that no workflow governs a page needs the space to say whether document control is on), PAG-148 (apps and report do not exist), PAG-150 (incoming links are inside Page information), COL-106 (chat), COL-108 (More of Share holds only the restrictions in detail), COL-117 (the title stays inside the sentence).

**For the frame (SC1).** The bell's button and its pressed state live in `components/notifications.tsx`, not in `topbar.tsx`; the panel is positioned under the bell by `together.css`. If the top bar changes its end group, check that the panel still hangs from the bell's end edge.

**For the product owner.** Decisions D1 to D11 in the spec are taken on your behalf and may be overruled. The two you asked about: the home page of a team space keeps its byline (D1), and six suggested statuses stay the limit (D2). Please look at the before and after list of pictures in the report.
