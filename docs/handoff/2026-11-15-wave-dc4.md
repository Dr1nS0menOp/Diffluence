# Handoff: wave DC4, electronic signatures (15 November 2026)

## State

- **Built:** wave DC4 of spec 064. A step of a workflow can require a signature with a meaning; signing takes a fresh confirmation for that one signature; the record is bound to the hash of the exact content signed, kept in the audit chain, shown on the page, in the history and in every export, and verified on demand, in the control history, in the auditor's export and by `diffluence signatures verify`. Signed records cannot be deleted or changed. The report is `docs/reports/2026-11-15-wave-dc4.md`; the decisions are DC4-1 to DC4-20 in the spec.

## For the product owner

DC4-1 to DC4-20 were decided on your behalf and may be overruled. Above all:

- DC4-3: a reviewer who signed an approval cannot change it into a request for changes;
- DC4-5: how an administrator identified a person is a choice of four, never free text, and nobody enables their own signing;
- DC4-7: a recovery code never signs;
- DC4-11: signed records are kept without end, the trash keeps a signed page for good, and pruning the audit log stops at the first signature;
- DC4-19 and DC4-20: the two open questions of the spec, answered as "not now" and "keep DOC-1417 for every controlled space".
