# Handoff: wave SC4, the administration (spec 081)

**Done.** The administration is rebuilt as blueprint 03 arranges it (ADR-048), steps 0 to 9 and 11 of spec 081:

- **Sidebar:** a header row with Back to the start screen and the name, which stays in place and gets a line once the list scrolls beneath it; Overview, Tools and People and access with icons, always open; a Settings heading with the jump field; Instance, Security, Permissions, Look and Data, which fold, stored per person under `admin-…` ids. The sidebar keeps its place between sections and shows a section opened by address or by the jump field. Automation and Import a space carry Experimental while on, and leave the sidebar while off.
- **Registry:** `web/src/views/admin-sections.ts` is the one list of sections, groups, widths, icons, experimental parts and documents. A new section is one line there, a name and an introduction in `en.json` (`adminSections.<id>`), and one case in `SectionContent` of `views/admin.tsx`; the unit test fails until all three exist.
- **Moves:** seven new addresses (`announcements`, `service-accounts`, `login`, `instance-permissions`, `space-defaults`, `import-space`, `spaces-trash`), and the font, categories, space templates and embed addresses moved into existing sections. Every old address still opens its section; Settings is General.
- **Components for SC5 to reuse:** `SettingsSection` with `SectionActions`, `Notice`, `SaveReset` (with `differs`), `EmptyState`, `TableEmpty`, and `GuideLink` with a `path`.
- **API:** spec 081, "Through the API", lists for every section the routes that read and change it and the rule each enforces. No route was missing, none was added.

No migration and no server change. Report: `docs/reports/2026-11-20-wave-sc4.md`.

**Not built, by decision:**

- Step 10, the tool rows of Users and the Audit log (ADM-103), moved to a wave of its own (D25). The dropdown filters stay.
- The empty block for Automation, Templates and Document control waits for SC5, which rebuilds those shared screens (D24).
- `.note` outside the administration keeps its look until each screen adopts `Notice` (D23).

**For the product owner to decide.** D21 to D30 in the spec are decided on your behalf and may be overruled. The ones that matter most:

- **D22:** every administration feature is usable through the API with a signed-in session; a personal access token still only reads, as RV10 decided. Letting a token (and so an MCP client) change the administration is a security decision for the spec that brings MCP.
- **D23:** the restyle of every `.note` in the app waits for each screen's own wave.
- **D25:** tool rows are a wave of their own.

**Next.** SC5, the settings of a space, can reuse the components above; its sidebar uses `SettingsFrame` with `back` and `tools` as the administration does, or a variant of them. Then the tool rows wave (ADM-103).

**Cleanup for later.** The administration no longer reads its old group ids (`overview`, `people`, `content`, `automation`, `look`, `system`) in `settings_shut`; the settings of a space still use `content` and `automation` for groups of their own, so those two keep folding there. Ids nobody reads stay in a person's list (at most 30) until it is written anew.
