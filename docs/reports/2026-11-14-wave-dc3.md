# Report: wave DC3, document control: the content around the page (14 November 2026)

## What happened

Wave DC3 is the third part of spec 064. Its scope is:

- DOC-500 to DOC-502, DOC-510 to DOC-512, DOC-520 and DOC-521;
- DOC-530 to DOC-532, DOC-540, DOC-550 to DOC-552, DOC-560 and DOC-570 to DOC-572;
- DOC-603 to DOC-607 and DOC-609;
- DOC-1253 and DOC-1302.

The blueprint marks each line built or partly. DOC-531, DOC-532 and DOC-551 are partly built: their signatures come with wave DC4. The decisions taken on the product owner's behalf are DC3-1 to DC3-25 in the spec.

## What was built

- **The one question.** `permissions.ReadsWorking` answers DOC-300 for a person and a page: whoever may edit it, and the reviewers of its open round in the stage the page is in, read the working version. Every door below asks it, directly or through `pages.VersionSeen`, and SQL lists use `perm_editable_pages` for the same rule.
- **Comments (DOC-500 to DOC-502).** A comment or reply written by someone who may edit, while a working version differs from the approved one, is kept for editors, as a thread made while editing was already. It reaches readers when a version that holds what it points at is approved; a reply in an open thread follows its version. A reviewer who may not edit cannot add a page comment then (`comment_in_review`), because they would not see a thread for editors. Resolving and deleting stay audited, and a review comment has no way to be deleted.
- **Files (DOC-510 to DOC-512).** Migration 0063 adds `attachment_pins`: each version of a controlled page pins the files it shows, with their version and name. `appendVersion` pins every new version of a controlled page, an assignment pins the version readers see and the head, and the migration pins the pages controlled before it.
  - A reader gets the pins of the version on the pages row everywhere a file can be had: the list of a page, a download, a version link, a text preview, a copy, a zip, the facts of a tile, the history of its files, exports and public sites.
  - A new version, an older version made current again, or a new name of a file the working version shows writes a new version of the page with the same text, through `attachments.ContentChanges`, which pages implements. Its review starts again, because the comparison with the approved version now includes the pins.
  - A file the approved version shows, or its pinned version, cannot be deleted, moved or removed (`file_approved`).
  - The Files screen, its filters and quick file search leave out, for a reader, a file of a pinned page that is not pinned as it is (`perm_unpinned_files`).
- **Search (DOC-520, DOC-521, DOC-1253, DOC-1302).** `search_working` holds the working title, text and file names of a controlled page while they differ from the approved version; approval, a return to the approved text and the removal of control drop it. A search asks it for the typed words, then the rule of editing for the pages found, and lists those first; the shown rows give an author the working title and excerpt. `search_names` gives readers the pinned names. Indexing an approval stays in its transaction, so readers find the version before until it commits. The spelling suggestion no longer reads titles of pages a reader may not see because they were never approved.
- **Exports and bundles (DOC-530 to DOC-532).** `pages.ForExport` gives each person the version they read, with an `ExportControl`. Every format puts a control line above a controlled page; a PDF that holds a working version says "Not approved" in the header of every page. Old versions in an HTML export follow the history rule. A bundle is version 8: `control.json` holds the workflows, where each page stands and its rounds with answers, reviewers and review comments; the pages hold the version readers saw; the files are those readers get. An import copies the workflows into the new space as page workflows, keeps the version readers saw, lands a page that waited for review in its start state, and stores the rounds marked with the installation they came from.
- **Public sites (DOC-540).** A site takes the files readers get, and rebuilds on `page.version_approved` as on a publish.
- **The API and webhooks (DOC-550 to DOC-552).** A token reads, steps and answers with the checks of the screens. Two webhook events, `page.version_approved` and `page.working_changed`; the second reaches only owners who may edit, checked at delivery.
- **Notifications (DOC-603 to DOC-607, DOC-609).**
  - A working version sends `page.working_changed`: watchers who may edit hear of it, and the people it newly mentions who may edit.
  - An approval sends `page.version_approved`: every watcher hears of it as the new kind "version approved", then the people whose mention was held, then those who asked for every approval in the space.
  - A notification row keeps `needs_edit`, and the list, the count and the mail ask the rule of editing for such rows.
  - The mail asks the rule itself: a reader of a controlled page gets no change but one between two approved versions, and no excerpt of a thread for editors.
  - The approvals digest is `approval_subscriptions`, with `GET`, `PUT` and `DELETE /api/v1/spaces/{id}/approvals-digest` and an item in the menu of a space. No new channel.
- **Templates (DOC-560).** A template of a space may name an active workflow of that space (`templates.workflow_id`). On GT1's set path it becomes the workflow of the first entry when that names none, before the check of rights; a space or branch workflow wins (`putNewPageUnderWorkflow` now asks `governingOf`, so a branch wins too).
- **The feed, analytics and the tree (DOC-570 to DOC-572).** The feed shows a controlled page approved by a person as "approved", at the time of the approval, in the recent and "for you" tabs. Analytics record the version a person read. The tree keeps showing the approved title.
- **Screens.** The menu of a space has "Tell me about every approval in this space". The template form has a choice of workflow when its space has one. Notifications have the kind "version approved", in the list and in the settings. The feed says "new version approved by".

## Load

The budgets of spec 064 for this wave are the feed within the budgets of wave PF plus a tenth (DOC-570) and a second search entry only for drafts in progress (DOC-1302). Measured on the real-data dump of wave PF with a tenth of the pages controlled, 80% of them approved by a person, and their files pinned (`docs/operations/limits.md` has the table). The machine ran other builds at a load of 11 to 13.

- **The feed.** A first design placed approvals at their own time beside the versions. On the real data it read every version: the recent feed took 857 ms against 150 ms allowed, and "for you" of the watcher of every page 12 s. It was replaced (DC3-23): the feed asks, for the rows of one page of it only, whether a person approved the page. Main and this wave, in turn under the same load: recent 72 and 47 ms against 59 and 48 ms; for you, member, 106 against 80 ms; for you, watcher of every page, 789 against 582 ms. That last one misses its 500 ms for both main and this wave on this machine; wave DC1 measured 390 ms on a quieter one.
- **Reads.** A controlled page took 10.5 ms against 8.1 ms for an ordinary one (5 ms allowed). The tree took 19 ms, the Files screen of the largest space 81 to 134 ms, quick search with files 42 to 73 ms, search on a common word 51 ms as an author and 69 ms as a reader. Seven approvals took 15.4 ms as the median and 26 ms at most (20 ms allowed for the median).
- **Search.** `search_working` held only drafts in progress: after the approvals of the run, no row. The tests prove that it has a row only while a draft differs, and none for a page never approved.
- **Queries.** Search costs one query more (the working entries that match, for pages the person may edit, in one query); its budget went from 19 to 20. A reader's file of a controlled page costs one query for the pins, and one for the rule when the page pins its files. A comment on a controlled page costs one query to know whether a working version differs.
- **Not measured:** `perm_unpinned_files` with many changed files waiting, and the notification list with many rows for editors; both ask the rule of editing only for the pages concerned.

## Checks

- **Tests first.** The tests of this wave are in `internal/app/control_around_test.go`. Several were written beside the code of their door rather than before it; every one was then run against the code of main with this wave's migration, and 20 of 24 failed there; the four that passed prove what was already held: the audit of resolving and deleting comments (DOC-502), indexing an approval in its transaction (DOC-1253), the API giving each caller its version (DOC-550), and the tree's approved title (DOC-572).
- **Failure and leak paths first.** Each test starts from a reader, and a guest where it matters, who must not see the working version: comments, replies and inline comments, comment search, a file's download, text preview, version link, version list, tile facts and history, the Files screen, its filters, quick search and search by file name, every export format and old versions in HTML, a bundle round trip, a public site, a token, a webhook endpoint of a reader, watchers, mentions, every notification kind rendered as a mail, the feed and analytics.
- **Tests changed:** the DC1 feed test now expects an approval item that keeps its time, text and title; the query budget of search went from 19 to 20; the real-data test marks approvals by a person and pins files.
- **`make check`:** passed on the tree rebased on main `18800f1`, with the 45-minute race limit of main. Before the rebase, `internal/app` under the race detector passed the old 20-minute limit at a machine load of about 32; alone with 60 minutes it passed apart from `TestARunningWorkerFollowsTheSwitchAndNotesTheDrain` of spec 068, which passed alone three times under the race detector: a timing test under load, not touched by this wave.
- **Boundary:** one new crossing, listed with its reason: the feed reads `page_control` for the approval label. Three new tables have owners: `attachment_pins` (attachments), `search_working` (search), `approval_subscriptions` (notify). Cross-module reads of pins and search names go through SQL functions in migration 0063.
- **Targeted mutation check:** `MUTATION_JOBS=2` with every changed path outside `internal/app`: 472 of 1068 ran. 458 were caught on the first run. Fourteen were not: seven older mutations aimed at lines this wave rewrote, three of them already broken since DC2 (the notification list joins `perm_review_pages`); five new ones left a variable unused; one aimed at feed code that was replaced; and two were missed. All fourteen were rewritten, the DC1 feed test and the mention test were made to see what they missed, and all fourteen were run again and caught. 22 mutations are new.
- **E2E:** `control.spec.ts` and `0-design.spec.ts` on the rebased tree, 108 passed, with `--update-snapshots`: no picture changed. The new test of `control.spec.ts` reads a page as a reader while a change and an author's remark wait, asks for every approval of the space from its menu, and names a workflow in a template, with the accessibility check on the template form. The new items are a menu entry, a choice in the template form that shows only when the space has a workflow, and a row at the end of the notification settings, below the part of the screen its picture holds; none of them is in a picture.
- **Web:** tsc, eslint, prettier, strings, design and vitest pass.
- **Landing on main `2fc73b8`** (after GT1, PB2, CS1 and RV7): `make check` passed with the sharded race run; the targeted mutation check of every changed path outside `internal/app` ran 507 mutations and caught all; `control`, `create-kinds`, `organising` and `0-design`, 151 passed with `--update-snapshots`, and no picture changed. The rebase added the draft-space column of PB to the rows search shows, and the test of DOC-1253 now releases its lock when it fails, so a failure cannot hang the run.

## Not proven in this wave

- Signing through a token is not refused here: there is no signing before DC4, which has its test (DOC-733).
- The PDF is read as the HTML the PDF helper receives, with its header file; the text of a real PDF from the helper is not read.
- A reviewer's review of a page whose file got a new version during the round: the round restarts, which is tested, but the reviewer's panel does not compare files.
- The reviewers a bundled workflow names keep the ids of the installation they came from; a space administrator names this installation's reviewers.
