# Report: wave SC2, the page (spec 079)

## What was asked

Rebuild the page as blueprint 03 describes it since the screens study of 2026-11-14 (ADR-045): the header row, the byline, More, Share and the panel of the bell (chapter 3, section 3.11, and chapter 7). The product owner confirmed on 2026-11-15 that the star and the lock leave the header, that the byline loses its date and version, and that Watch becomes a sub-menu.

## What changed

- **Header row** (PAG-140 to PAG-143, PAG-164): the first row of the page, wider than the reading column, so its end stands outside it. Breadcrumbs at the start; at the end the publishing state with the date of the last update, who else is reading, Edit, Share with the lock on it joined to Copy link, and More. The home page of a space has the space alone in its path and a line under the row; a personal home page has no publishing state and no byline (PAG-146).
- **Byline** (PAG-144, PAG-145, COL-099): owner, view count, Add a reaction (the same short list and picker as at the bottom), the state of a controlled page (which opens the review panel for whoever may), the marks and the status. Read time, version, date, the count of comments and the Restricted mark left it; read time and versions are in Details.
- **More** (PAG-148 to PAG-151): groups divided by lines, sub-menus for Watch (three choices, the eye filled while any is on), More details, Convert, Export (per format), Run a rule and Archive or delete; nothing red. Favourite is a checkbox entry with a short message on change; `s` and `w` work as before. `MenuSub` brings the keys of the menu pattern and a placement that stays on a 360-pixel screen and mirrors in right-to-left.
- **Share** (COL-102, COL-103, COL-105 to COL-108): a card near the top with More and Close in its title row; one field for people, groups and, for those who may invite, e-mail addresses (sent as invitation requests with the message as the note); General access as two plain choices that give the three states of ACC-064 and are words without *manage restrictions*; the access list only when it has entries; Copy link and the single shared page at the foot; Send only once someone is named. The restrictions dialog and Share share one hook for the state.
- **Notification panel** (COL-113 to COL-118): fixed height with its own scrolling, the bell pressed with its number hidden, title with the Only unread switch, a button to all notifications and More with Mark all as read; tabs For me and Watched; a heading per day; the kind of content and the space under each item; the start of a comment in a quiet frame; the unread mark as a button at the inner end. Escape closes it and returns to the bell. All notifications has the same tabs.
- **Server**: `tab` and `excerpts` on the list, `tab` on Mark all as read, `page_kind` on each item. A comment or reaction on the person's own page is For me even though they watch it. The excerpt is read in one query for all items, through the editable pages for threads kept for editors, and never from a removed comment.

## Decisions (on the product owner's behalf, 2026-11-15, may be overruled)

D1 to D11 are in the spec. In short: a team space's home page keeps its byline (D1); six suggested statuses stay the limit (D2); built entries of More that the lines do not name keep a place (D3), Comments among them until page tools exist; the single shared page moves to Share (D4); the title stays inside a notification's sentence (D9); replying and reacting from the panel, folding updates and suggested people wait (D10, D11). ADR-047 records what is superseded.

## Gates

All gates ran on 2026-11-16 and 17 in the development containers, one heavy gate at a time. The pictures, the end-to-end suite and `make check` ran again after the rebase onto SC1 (2b4c23f).

- `make check` (two shards for `internal/app`): passed, `internal/permissions` included. The list of tables each module may read now names `comments` and `pages` for notify, with the reason: the excerpt query of the panel, and the person's own pages for the For me tab.
- The design pictures: taken again with the baselines deleted, then `0-design.spec.ts` passed twice in a row, 134 of 134, across midnight.
- The full end-to-end suite, after the rebase: 550 passed, 8 skipped, 2 failed, both from this wave:
  - The second press of `s` read the page from before the first one, because the message came before the page was read again. Favourite now announces itself only once the page has been read again, and ignores `s` while a change is under way.
  - The Share test asked the server while its two saves were still on their way. It now waits for them.

  `reading.spec.ts` and `together.spec.ts` then passed, 52 of 52.
- Before the rebase, the full suite caught "In review" twice on the page in `control.spec.ts` and `publishing.spec.ts`, because the byline now shows the workflow state. Both now look in the byline.
- The rebase:
  - **`icons.tsx`:** SC1 and this wave both added `IconStarFilled`. One is kept, made the same way as the filled eye.
  - **`menu.tsx`:** keeps SC1's placement of floating lists and this wave's sideways shift. Its arrow keys move among the entries of the list that holds the focus, so they stay inside an open sub-menu.
  - **`layout.css`:** mirrors only the sub-menu arrow in right-to-left, because SC1 dropped the sidebar toggle from that rule.
  - **`together.css`:** drops the start-screen columns that SC1 removed.
- The mutation check, before the rebase (the rebase changed no Go code of this wave): every mutation ran, because the script changed. 1319 ran and all were caught. The first full run left 4 uncaught, which led to these fixes:
  - **Lost newline:** one of this wave's new lines had lost its newline. "The notification cursor skips what shares a time" ran with a test name that matched nothing, and "the panel shows a thread kept for editors to a reader" never ran. Both now run and are caught.
  - **Mis-aimed upgrade mutations:** "a restart counts as an upgrade" and "a refusal is a plain error a node cannot wait on" changed the first match in `upgrade.go`. Since wave RV3b added `waitForMigrations`, that first match lies in `waitForMigrations` instead of `checkBeforeUpgrade`. Each now names text that only `checkBeforeUpgrade` holds, and its test catches it again.
  - **A test that no longer sees the bug:** "a new space reads the instance through a second connection" survived. `TestManySpacesCreatedAtOnceAllFinish` no longer runs out of connections, even when a new space asks the pool for a second one. A new test in `internal/features` gives a pool of one connection to a transaction that holds it, so the second request can only wait. The mutation now runs against that test.

  During the run, the disk kept more than 400 GB free; the Go build cache was cleared afterwards.

## What is not proven

- How the panel reads with many notifications from several days and spaces at once: the tests hold a few.
- The placement of a sub-menu was tested on a wide screen, at 360 pixels and in right-to-left, not on every size between, and not with a menu that scrolls inside a short window.
- The excerpt adds one query when the panel opens; it was not measured under load. The tab condition adds a lookup of the person's own pages; not measured on the real-data dump.
- Screen readers were not tried by hand; axe ran on the page, the sub-menu, Share and the panel.
- The look of the switch and the joined Share control was checked in the pictures only, in light and dark where a picture has both.
