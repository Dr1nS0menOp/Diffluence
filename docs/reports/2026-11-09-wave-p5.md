## Nobody loses access through this wave

Checked by `TestEveryWayToAdministerASpaceGivesTheFinerPermissions` and the bundle tests:

- The built-in Space admin role holds all eight. It is given at space creation, for a personal space, at its handover, and to whoever imports a bundle.
- The migration gives the eight to every role and old set that held administration, and the grants of their holders follow.
- Bundles of format 1 and 2 give them with administration.
- The older grants route (`PUT /spaces/{id}/grants`) gave exactly the list it got, so a list from before the split would have lost the eight. Fixed: `permissions.WithSplitOff` completes such a list, the preview does the same, and two mutations guard it.
- Instance tools and Markdown or Confluence imports give no space permissions. Only a role made by hand after this wave holds administration without the eight, and the role editor shows them beside it.
