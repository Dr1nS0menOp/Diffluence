# Report: wave PF, reads fast on real data (12 November 2026)

## What happened

The second stress test found the feed not answering within a minute on the seeded data of the server, after spec 050 had measured it at 20 ms on generated data. This wave copied a dump of that data off the server and measured every hot read on it, then fixed what the plans showed. Spec 061 has the plans, the causes and the decisions.

## Results, on the real data, nothing else running

| Feed | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Recent, member | 233 ms, or over 2 minutes with a generic plan | 43 ms |
| Recent, watcher of every page | 79 ms | 35 ms |
| For you, member | over 2 minutes | 73 ms |
| For you, watcher of every page | over 2 minutes | 233 ms |
| Popular | about 320 ms | 107 to 187 ms |

The page tree (36 ms), the storage still free (3 ms), the bell (0.2 ms) and the spaces a person may see (6.6 ms) were already fast alone. In the stress test they waited for cores the feed held.

## Causes

- "For you" read every version to find the few a person watches.
- pgx's prepared statements met generic plans after five runs, which cannot see empty optional filters.
- The visibility rule was worked out again for every candidate.
- JIT compiled plans for half a second or more.
- "Popular" counted page by page.

## What changed

- The pool plans each query with its values and does not JIT.
- Every query of a read stops after 15 seconds, answering "busy".
- "For you" gathers watched and followed pages first.
- The visibility rule is built once per query as a hash.
- "Popular" counts in one pass.
- `scripts/stress/real-data.sh` and `TestTheFeedIsFastOnRealData` repeat the measurement on any dump.

## Checks

- `make check`: passed.
- Targeted mutation check: 470 run, all caught, 11 of them new.
  - Four existing mutations of wave LD aimed at lines this wave changed; they now aim at the new lines.
  - One new mutation did not compile and was rewritten.
- `0-design.spec.ts`: 96 passed.
- `TestTheFeedIsFastOnRealData` against the dump: every tab within its budget.

## Not proven

The load test on the server with these changes, a guest (the data has none), and the step from the middleware to the 503 at full load.
