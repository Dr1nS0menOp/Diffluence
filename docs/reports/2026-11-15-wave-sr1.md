Builder report for the first search wave of spec 080. It covers which lines were taken, what was found, built, measured and proven.

## 2. Leak paths checked first

Before building, each way search answers was checked for what it could tell a reader about a page they may not see, with a test written first and seen failing on the code of main:

| Path | On main | Now |
|---|---|---|
| The total of a search | A controlled page the reader may not see (never approved, or withdrawn) was left out of the rows but counted: a word only in such a page gave a total of 1 and no rows | The rule is asked about every picked row, and the total counts what it lets through |
| The way back under a result, and on the page | Every ancestor was named, without asking the rule; on the real data 1 of 61 ancestors of a sample of results was such a page | Only ancestors the reader may see, on the result and on the page itself |
| Below a page | Narrowing to a page the reader may not see listed the pages under it that they may see | Nothing is found, as if the page did not exist |
| The home mark | Not there | `home` on each result |

Unchanged and checked: suggestions (titles the reader may see), filters without words and their count (`perm_visible_pages`), comments (`perm_visible_comments`), files, spaces and people in the box.

## 3. What was built

- **Server** (`internal/search`, `internal/pages`): `pickedRows` asks `perm_visible_pages` about every picked row (at most 201) instead of the shown 25, makes excerpts for the shown rows only, and the total counts what it returns; the breadcrumbs, of a result and of the page screen, mark ancestors that are controlled pages waiting for a first approval or withdrawn, and ask `perm_control_hidden_pages` about those only when there is one; `belowHidden` answers a search below a hidden page with nothing; `Result.Home`.
- **First attempt, measured and dropped:** adding `perm_control_hidden_pages` to the picking's hidden set and `perm_visible_pages` to the breadcrumb query. On the real data each asked the rule of control once more per search, and with the VM loaded that cost about 60 to 110 ms and 130 to 150 ms more per search, against 4 ms for the old breadcrumb query.
- **Results screen** (`web/src/views/search.tsx`): the mark of each result's kind; the screen of a search that found nothing, with its ways forward as links in the text and a guide link; the loading box; a field that chooses the page to search below; several people as chips.
- **Search box** (`components/quick-search.tsx`): each row starts with the mark of its kind; a file's kind from its name, a space's badge, a person's initials.
- **General parts:** `KindMark` and `FileMark` (`components/kind-mark.tsx`), `LoadingBox`, `GuideLink`, and two icons of our own for a document and a PDF.

## 4. Load

The query budget of a results page stays 20. Measured on the real-data dump (60,385 pages, a tenth under control by `markControlled`), as the median of seven requests through the whole application, for a reader of the largest space, on the build VM; main and SR1 were run one after the other on the same database:

| Search | main, run 1 | SR1, run 1 | main, run 2 | SR1, run 2 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A common word | 642 ms | 645 ms | 578 ms | 722 ms |
| A common word, newest first | 754 ms | 914 ms | 786 ms | 842 ms |
| A rare word | 722 ms | 509 ms | 451 ms | 723 ms |
| A typo | 881 ms | 580 ms | 579 ms | 660 ms |
| Nothing found | 684 ms | 651 ms | 613 ms | 698 ms |
| A label alone | 3.02 s | 3.24 s | 3.97 s | 3.10 s |
| Below a page | 1.12 s | 1.39 s | 1.12 s | 685 ms |
| Two people | 814 ms | 825 ms | 546 ms | 806 ms |
| The box | 1.64 s | 1.59 s | 955 ms | 1.55 s |
| The box, a typo | 1.30 s | 1.40 s | 1.40 s | 792 ms |

The VM ran at a load of 30 to 38 on 7 cores while these ran, after a restart of its host, so every number is many times what the stress server measured (28 ms for a common word, spec 064) and the spread between two runs of the same code is as large as any difference between main and SR1. What they show is that SR1 adds no cost that stands out of that noise. They do not prove the budget of 100 ms per search that `TestSearchOnRealData` holds; that needs a quiet machine.

Measured directly in the database before the rework, same load: the old breadcrumb query 3 to 6 ms; with `perm_visible_pages` 139 to 228 ms; `perm_control_hidden_pages` for one reader 98 to 125 ms, whether asked about all pages or about 201, which is why the rule is now asked once per search and the breadcrumbs ask it only when a marked ancestor is there.

## 5. How the tests prove it

| Test | Proves |
|---|---|
| `TestASearchCountsOnlyPagesTheReaderMaySee` | For a restriction and for document control, a hidden page is neither listed nor counted, by text, title, near words, date, titles only and filters; paging neither skips nor repeats; the author counts what they may see |
| `TestSearchBreadcrumbsNameOnlyPagesTheReaderMaySee` | The way back never names a hidden page; the author keeps it |
| `TestSearchBelowAPageTheReaderMayNotSeeFindsNothing` | Below a hidden page nothing is found, with words, without words and with filters; below an open page its branch is found |
| `TestSearchResultsSayWhichPageIsAHomePage` | `home` and `kind` on results |
| `TestTheMainScreensStayWithinTheirQueryBudget` (unchanged) | 20 queries for a results page |
| `TestSearchOnRealData` | Each search under 100 ms on the real-data dump |
| `kind-mark.test.ts`, `search.test.ts`, `reading.test.ts` | The kinds of pages and files; filters in the address; the wider searches of an empty result; the marks of the box |

## 6. Gates

All on main `0348fa9` with this wave on top. The build VM was lost twice while the gates ran, so they ran on the development PC, one at a time and at low priority.

- `make check` with `TEST_SHARDS=2`: passed; `internal/permissions` 1265 s, the two shards of `internal/app` 624 s and 704 s. A first run found two things, both repaired: the module boundary list needed the new mentions of `pages` and `page_control` by `internal/search`, and `TestASearchThatFindsNoSlotHearsThatItIsBusy` failed once under load, because `enter` let a timer of 1 ms win over a free slot. A free slot is now taken before the timer starts.
- Targeted mutation check, `MUTATION_JOBS=2`, with the changed paths of `internal/search`, `internal/pages`, `internal/db`, `scripts/stress` and the web (not `internal/app`, not the script): 206 run, 201 caught. The five left were three search mutations of earlier waves that no longer fit, because the rule now stands in `pickedRows` for the count as well (one no longer applied, two took away only the narrowing that is now for speed), and two of this wave that did not compile. All five were repaired, the older three now take away the narrowing and the rule together, and run alone with a copy of the script all five were caught.
- e2e `tree-search.spec.ts` and `0-design.spec.ts`: 139 passed on the PC; on the build VM, with the spelling fix below, 136 passed and one picture of another screen (the history of a released draft page, whose columns moved by a few pixels) differed, as it did once on the PC before passing twice there on the same code, so it is counted as a flake of that picture and not retaken. A first run showed that `.quick-row` is shared with the people picker, the labels and the template gallery, which the mark had turned into a row; the search box now has its own modifier, `with-mark`. axe then asked for room between the title of a result and its way back (WCAG 2.5.8): both links have a target of 24 px.
- The new picture of an empty search showed that a similar spelling took the filter `label:` for words ("handbook label nowhere"). A suggestion now covers the words to find only (`wordsToSpell`), and its link keeps the filter words; tests in `internal/search`, `internal/app` and the web, and a mutation, caught alone. After the fix the search tests, the query budget and that mutation ran on the build VM; `make check` ran in full before it and, after it, up to the race tests, when the build host went down.
- Pictures retaken and looked at: `search`, `search-filters`, `quick-search` and `quick-search-filters` show the mark of each kind before the title; `search-empty` is new. No other picture changed.

## Decisions taken on the product owner's behalf, may be overruled

D1 to D7 of spec 080: the scope of SR1; the way back leaves out a hidden ancestor; below a hidden page finds nothing; an empty result offers only wider searches that change something; a file's kind from its name; `LoadingBox` and `GuideLink` as general parts; "A person" for a profile the reader may not see.

## Not proven

- The load numbers are from an overloaded build VM; they compare main and SR1 on the same database, not capacity, and the 100 ms budget of `TestSearchOnRealData` is not yet shown on a quiet machine.
- Paging past the first 200 results: the picking looks a little past the page, and when hidden controlled pages are among the picked rows, the last page of such a search can come back shorter than a full page, so the screen stops offering more there. For the same reason a total just under 200 can be too low rather than say "more than": decided so on purpose, because counting picked rows there would let hidden pages lift the count.
- The People and Spaces tabs of the results still show the plain sentence when they find nothing (SR2).
- The loading box was looked at in a picture only through the code: it shows for a moment, and no design picture holds it.
