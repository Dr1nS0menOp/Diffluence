# Wave PB2: draft and published spaces, the whole space and the release screen

Builder report for spec 073, wave PB2. It covers what was built, what was measured, what was decided on the product owner's behalf, and what is not proven yet. The part stays experimental and off by default.

## What was built

**Runs of more than one page.** A run takes a branch, the whole space, pages chosen on the release screen, or pages to take off. Each starts as a preview (`POST /spaces/{id}/releases/preview`) that keeps, per page, the version it will release, what it does (new, changed, moved or restyled, taken off, archived) and why a page stays out (left out, below a page left out, restricted in the draft, never released, archived, never approved). The preview counts pages hidden from the releaser without their titles, the files to copy against the published space's room, the links that will stay words, and the number of batches. Confirming it (`/start`) queues the run for the worker; the rights are checked again at the start and before every batch.

A run of up to 1,000 pages, estimated within 30 seconds, is one transaction. A larger one first makes the twins of every page it releases for the first time, without a version, in a short transaction of their own, so links of an early batch lead to pages of a later one; then it writes batches of 250, each its own transaction. A batch that fails leaves nothing of itself; the run stops with what was done, shows as stopped, and resumes with the versions of its preview. A run whose node died shows as stopped once its sign of life is two minutes old. A run can be cancelled while it waits or between batches; the twins it made for pages it did not write go. One run per pair at a time; at most two per node. A release of one page, and ending a pair, are refused while a larger run waits or goes.

**Leaving out and taking off.** A page is left out alone or with every page below it (`PUT /pages/{id}/leave-out`), with a reason. New pages below follow the mark and a page moved out of the branch leaves it, because the mark is read through the tree. A page left out cannot be released alone, and its twin, if it has one, stays as it is (Left out, still published). Taking off sends twins to the published space's trash, never purges them, moves twins below that stay to the nearest twin above, and ends the page pairs; releasing again makes a new twin, and nobody restores the old one from the trash while the pair stands.

**Mirroring the draft.** A whole-space release takes off the twins of pages deleted, moved to another space or purged, archives the twins of archived pages, and unarchives them when the draft page comes back. A deleted or archived page keeps its page pair; a moved or purged one loses it, and its twin waits in `release_removals` until it is taken off. Deleting either space ends its pair; linking the same two spaces again restores their page pairs, and a twin edited by hand meanwhile is marked changed.

**Out of step, completed.** The trigger of PB1 now also keeps: the order among siblings (D24: a page is out of place when both its neighbours among the released siblings differ from its twin's, so a move marks the moved page alone), files (a newer version or a file gone in the draft, also kept by a trigger on `attachments`), links waiting (a page whose link became words now has a target with a twin), and a page back from the archive. Every run orders the twins it wrote after the twin of the sibling before their draft page, and compares the whole group again.

**The release screen.** `Release` in the sidebar of a draft space, for those with `space.release`: tiles with counts, tabs To release (oldest change first), Left out, Removed in the draft and History, a table with a real checkbox per row and the tint on chosen rows, Compare on changed pages, the primary button "Release N pages…" or "Release everything that changed…", "Release a branch…" with the page chooser, and Leave out, Include and Take off in a menu on the choice. The preview opens in a dialog with the counts as a sentence, each list in a section that folds, the files against the room, the warnings, the note for readers and Tell watchers. A run that waits, goes or stopped shows its progress as text in a status line, with Cancel and Resume. History lists the runs newest first, each opening its report. The page's More menu gains Leave out of releases…, Include in releases again and Take off the published space…; the band on a draft page shows Left out, and the comments readers left on the twin as a count with a link.

**Told once.** A version written by a run of many pages tells mentioned people, never watchers; the run then tells each watcher of its pages or of the published space once, on the published home page, with how many of the pages they may see it changed or took off. A silent run tells nobody but the releaser, who is told every run's end. The feed shows a run as one item on the published home page with its note and the number of its pages the reader may see; its pages do not show one by one. Twins keep no task rows. Webhooks gain `page.released` and `release.finished`. Search results of a draft space carry the word Draft.

**History and checks.** `GET /releases/history`; `GET /releases/as-of?at=&page_id=` answers which twin version readers had at a moment and which draft version it came from; `GET /releases/export?format=csv|jsonl` exports each run and page with both sides' versions and content hashes, for those with `space.audit`, and is audited. `POST /releases/check` and `diffluence check publishing [--fix]` recompute every status, compare the facts of each release with its version, list twin versions no release wrote, and fix the statuses when asked. `GET /publishing/linking-in` lists pages of other spaces that link into the draft space; `GET /publishing/end-preview` says what ending leaves.

**Data.** Migration 0064 (0063 holds the number of DC3): `release_exclusions`, `release_removals`, indexes on `page_pairs` by pair and change and on its text links, new columns and wider checks on `release_runs` and `release_run_pages`, a new `page_pair_refresh`, and triggers on `attachments`, on deleting and moving pages and on trashing spaces. Expand only.

## Tests

Written against the behaviour of the spec, failure and leak paths first. The suite was written alongside the code rather than strictly before it, so a failing first run is not the evidence that each test can fail: the mutations are. Each new check has a mutation in `scripts/mutation-check.sh` under "Spec 073 (wave PB2)", and the run of those mutations is the proof that a test catches it.

- `internal/app/publishing_runs_test.go`, 18 tests. The leak test of PB1 extended to a whole-space and a branch release: the published space, its tree, every twin's doors, search, the quick search, both feeds and the bell, rendered for a reader of the published space, hold no id, key or name of the draft space, no page left out or restricted in the draft and no word only the draft has; the release screen and its runs do not exist for that reader. It found a real leak while it was being written: the draft home page carries the draft space's name as its title, which a release would give readers (D28).
- Rights on every new route; restricted pages out of bulk runs unless named and hidden ones counted without titles; leaving out by tree, both answers for a released page, including again; taking off without purging and a new twin after; the four removals and both ways back; sibling order with released and unreleased siblings and only the moved page marked; links within one run both ways and links waiting; a batched run failing inside its second batch and resuming with the previewed versions; lost rights, cancel and the refusal of a single release during a run; storage, size and a missing file object; the consistency check; one feed item and one notification per person per run, silent runs and task rows; the history at four moments and both exports; ending, relinking after edits on both sides, archiving and deleting a space; the screen's order and tiles; a new version of a file.
- `internal/publishing/runs_test.go`: what a run does with each kind of page as a table, placing twins among siblings, and when a run goes in batches.
- `internal/app/publishing_budget_test.go`: the measurements below.
- `web/e2e/publishing.spec.ts`: a whole-space release from the release screen by keyboard, with a page left out from its More menu; `web/e2e/0-design.spec.ts`: the release screen, its preview and its history.

## Measurements

Measured in the development toolbox on generated data (a handbook of 40 sections of 49 pages), on a machine loaded by other builds at the same time. The real-data dump of wave PF was not on this machine, so PUB-1427 and PUB-1108 are not measured.

| What | Budget | Measured |
|---|---|---|
| Whole-space release, 2,001 pages, 9 batches | 50 pages a second (PUB-1105) | 74 s, 27 pages a second |
| Whole-space release, 981 pages, one transaction | 30 s, 50 pages a second (PUB-1105, PUB-1106) | 39 s, 25 pages a second |
| Preview of 2,001 pages | 2 s for 1,000 (PUB-1104) | 150 to 190 ms |
| Second whole-space preview, nothing to do | 2 s (PUB-1108) | 25 ms |
| Release screen at 5,001 pages, over HTTP | 100 ms (PUB-1103) | 61 ms best, 68 to 107 ms first |
| A page of a run, statements | about a publish | 59 statements against 41 for a publish; 31 ms against 15 ms here |

A run is about twice the cost of publishing the same pages on this machine, where a statement of a run takes 0.5 ms against 0.37 ms for a publish. Reading the gate of a batch once instead of per page, writing look and labels only when they changed, comparing the order by index and leaving the comparison of a written page to the end of its batch took it from 71 statements and 34 ms a page to 59 and 31 ms. The budget of 50 pages a second is not met here, and the one-transaction run of 981 pages lasts past 30 s; the estimate now counts 30 ms a page (D35), so a run that would last longer goes in batches. Whether the server reaches the budget is for its next stress test.

## Gates

- `make check` on main `d0e6ec4` (RV6, the space strip fix and GT1): green. `internal/app` took 2,195 s under the race detector, within its timeout of 45 minutes. On an earlier base, under a load average of 20 to 27, `TestARunningWorkerFollowsTheSwitchAndNotesTheDrain` failed twice by reading the cursors of the event log a moment before the fourth listener had passed; alone it passed three times out of three, and it passed in the final run. This wave adds no listener.
- e2e on `d0e6ec4`: `0-design.spec.ts` and `publishing.spec.ts`, 108 passed. New pictures `publishing-screen`, `publishing-run-preview` and `publishing-history`; changed, and looked at: `publishing-settings` (the list of pages that link here), `publishing-draft-page` and `publishing-release` (the Release link in the sidebar of a draft space).
- Mutations: `MUTATION_CHANGED` with the changed Go and SQL paths outside `internal/app`, `MUTATION_JOBS=2`, on `d0e6ec4`: 400 run, 394 caught. Of the six left: "a batch commits half" could not commit half as first written, since a failed statement ends its transaction anyway, so it now also gives every page a savepoint; "a silent run tells watchers" and "a page moved away keeps its pair" showed two tests too weak (the list puts the rows of one run on one line, and a page moved away and back was not checked), which now count rows in the table and check the move back; and three mutations of `internal/notify` that already failed on main (two "no longer applies", one that changed nothing) aimed at the join of `list.go` as it was before wave DC2, and now aim at it as it is. Each of the six was then applied by hand and caught by its test, with the suite compiling. The script's own summary line was lost because the script was edited while it ran.

## Upgrade impact

Migration 0064 is expand only: two new tables, new columns with defaults, wider checks, two indexes on `page_pairs`, and functions and triggers that do nothing outside a pair. Nothing changes until an administrator turns the part on. Runs of more than one page are background work that only nodes of this release know (a node of the previous release leaves the job for one that does, OPS-070), so finish upgrading every node before starting one. Migration 0063 only holds the number of wave DC3.

## Decided on the product owner's behalf on 2026-11-14

D23 to D38 in the decision table of spec 073, each "decided on the product owner's behalf on 2026-11-14, may be overruled": the preview always in the request; the order among siblings by neighbours; a single release or the end of a pair refused during a run; pages below a page left out; who resumes a run; the title of a released home page; how a run tells people; how removals are kept and spaces' deletes end pairs; relinking; no task rows on twins; the tile for approval; the webhook events; the estimate of a run; and the spec's three open questions: keep "release", one published space per draft space, and the part off at 1.0 unless it has run on a real installation by then.

## Not proven, and not built

- **Real data and the server.** No budget was measured on the real-data dump with its largest space paired, or on the server; the run rate here is half the budget.
- **Not built:** the export of the release history carries no positions in the audit chain (PUB-913 in part); the weekly self-check and the button on the health page for the consistency check (PUB-343 in part); a preview in the background (D23); a single release queued behind a run instead of refused (D25); the draft space's feed showing runs to releasers (PUB-811); webhook deliveries to the draft space's subscriptions with both sides' ids (PUB-830 in part); the stopped run told to watchers of the draft space with `space.release` (PUB-822 in part); bundles carrying the marks left out (PUB-1042, PUB-1043); audit retention of a pair under document control (PUB-904); "released as version N" in the draft page's history (PUB-911, PUB-1223); the tree filter Changed since release (PUB-1224); the note for would-be editors on a twin (PUB-1231); the end of a pair told to the other side's administrators when a space is deleted (PUB-1046, PUB-1047); the restore drill step with a pair and a run going (PUB-1032).
- **Not tested on their own:** mentions told once in a twin (PUB-812), digests (PUB-815), search during a batched run (PUB-803), a failing mail sender (PUB-1066), a third run waiting on one node (PUB-1105), tokens releasing (PUB-832), moving a page into a draft space from another pair (PUB-1002, PUB-1003), importing into the draft space (PUB-1040), deleting the draft space (PUB-1047), a 1 GB file's memory (PUB-1107), restrictions on a twin kept across releases beyond what the code shows (PUB-222).
