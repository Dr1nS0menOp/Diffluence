# Report: wave SC1, the frame (spec 078)

## What was asked

Rebuild the frame as blueprint 03 now arranges it, after the study of 2026-11-14 (ADR-045). The scope was chapter 1, sections 1.2, 1.3 and 1.10 to 1.15, and chapter 2, section 2.4: the top bar, the global part of the sidebar and its panels, the space's block in the sidebar, and the start screen. The product owner confirmed on 2026-11-15 that this is wanted, including the parts that reverse ADR-027 and spec 033. ADR-046 records it.

## What was built

| Group | Lines | What changed |
|---|---|---|
| Top bar | NAV-090 to NAV-098 | Three groups. Start: the sidebar button and the name. Middle: search, then Create. End: bell, help, settings and account, as round icon buttons. ## Decided on the product owner's behalf (may be overruled)

These are D1 to D16 in the spec. The ones a person notices first:

- **D1:** People is an entry of the sidebar, because the directory had no other way in once the top bar lost it.
- **D2:** Templates waits for a screen that lists the templates a person may use.
- **D3:** Drafts is a screen of its own.
- **D4:** There is no sidebar button on screens without a sidebar that hides.
- **D7:** The Recent panel has three groups (Visited, Worked on, Drafts).
- **D8:** Import in the Spaces panel goes to the bundles of the administration.
- **D10:** Links to other tools go in one place, the top bar, because it is on every frame and every width.
- **D11:** Shortcuts show to readers only when there are some.
- **D13:** Until the create menu of section 2.5 is built, the Content add button offers New page, New database and Add a link.
- **D14:** The approvals digest stays in the space menu.

## What is not proven

- No screen reader was used; the roles, names and focus order are proven by the browser tests and axe only.
- Dragging an entry in Customise sidebar is tested by keyboard (Move up), not by mouse dragging.
- The panel's half of SPC-044 (dropping a page on a space) is covered by the existing drag test, which now goes through the panel; dropping a page on a space in the panel on a touch screen is not tested.
- Back to top is tested by scrolling the sidebar with a script, not with a wheel or a touch.
- The before and after pictures were compared by eye; the feed cards were seen only with the design data, not with long excerpts or many reactions.
