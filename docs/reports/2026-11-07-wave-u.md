# Report: wave U, 7 November 2026

## Scope

The open lines of blueprint 03 that do not wait for the product owner. The study in spec 030 found most open lines to belong to the look of ADR-034, to helpers and calendars, or to waves of their own (templates, databases, editor details). This wave built seven lines that are clear and small enough to test well.

## Decisions taken in the wave

- The guest list shows space keys, not names, so the column stays short.
- The overview of a person shows a count of pages owned, never titles, because administrators read no content by default.
- The disk warning uses `statfs` on Unix and stays silent elsewhere, and for object storage.

## Not proven

- The disk warning was tested on its threshold only, not on a really full disk.
- EDT-150 was not measured on a slow device, or for the row of tools on its own.
