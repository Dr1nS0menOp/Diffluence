# Wave GT4: guided templates, rights per template and approval

Builder report for spec 074, wave GT4. The study is `docs/blueprint/09-enterprise/templates/`, chapter 07 with chapters 01, 08, 09, 10, 11, 12 and 13; the decisions taken while building are TPL-1477 to TPL-1497 in the spec's decision table.

## What was built

**Who has a template** (TPL-500 to TPL-504, `internal/templates/access.go`, `rights.go`). What one person is to one template is read once, as a `standing`: whether they read its space, manage its templates, may propose templates, run the space, own it, and whether its two lists let them. Every decision about a template is a method on it: `manages`, `mayEdit`, `mayLook`, and `maySee`. A person who may not see a template is answered "not found" on every route; one who may see it and may not do what they asked, "forbidden". The lists only narrow: the users list is one more condition after `page.create`, and the editors list one more after `space.templates` (or administrator for a template of all spaces, whom no list locks out). An editors list that leaves out the person setting it is refused (`editors_lockout`); whoever runs the space may always clear the lists, and the audit says so. A template of all spaces can be offered in chosen spaces only; a deleted space drops off with its row. A template with a list or chosen spaces counts as one that uses the guided part, so it is not offered while the part is off.

**The gallery** stays one query (TPL-900): the users list and the chosen spaces are `EXISTS` tests in its `WHERE`, with the person's groups passed in, and the owner's own draft is offered to them alone, marked Draft.

**States and versions** (TPL-510, TPL-522 to TPL-524, `versions.go`, `offer.go`). A version is draft, waiting, approved, sent back, superseded or withdrawn; the template shows the state of its newest version and offers `approved_version`. Saving decides what the new version becomes: approved at once for whoever manages the template where no second person is asked for, otherwise a draft that leaves the approved version in use. A save after a proposal ends it. Whether pages may be made from a version is one function, `usableFor`: the approved version; a superseded one for a day after the approval that replaced it, for a form already open; a draft or a sent-back version for its owner, whose pages are then drafts whatever the template says. Waiting and withdrawn versions make nothing, for anyone. The set's transaction reads the version again with `FOR SHARE` and asks the users list again, so a withdrawal commits before the set or after it (TPL-503, TPL-524).

**Approval** (TPL-511, TPL-520 to TPL-525, `approval.go`). `Propose`, `Approve`, `SendBack` and `Withdraw`, each in one transaction with the rows of the template and the version held, so two decisions at once make one (TPL-855). Approval runs every check of a save again (TPL-525). Where four eyes are on, the author of a version never decides on it (`self_approval`); where they are off, a manager may approve their own, as they could publish it directly. A withdrawal takes the approved version and any replaced one out of use at once. Every proposal and decision is kept in `template_reviews` with its comment or reason, written to the audit (`template.proposed`, `template.approved`, `template.sent_back`, `template.withdrawn`, `template.rights_changed`, `template.setting_changed`) and the outbox, in the transaction of the change.

**Proposers** (TPL-061, TPL-512, TPL-1002, TPL-1102). `template.propose` is split off `page.create` like every earlier split: in the rule, the matrix, the role templates, the words, the migration (`rbac_split_roles_from`) and bundles (version 9). A person with it makes drafts at `POST /spaces/{id}/my-templates`, lists their own at `GET`, edits and proposes them, and deletes one never approved.

**Four eyes** (TPL-511, TPL-521). `template_four_eyes` holds the setting per space, and for templates of all spaces in the row without a space. Whoever runs the space turns it on and off (an administrator for all spaces); the screen says when nobody else could approve.

**Notifications** (TPL-720 to TPL-723, `internal/notify/templates.go`). Three new types. A proposal tells who may approve it, at most fifty, never its author; a decision tells the author; a withdrawal the author and the managers; the first decision marks the proposal read for everyone. These rows hang on the template's hidden page and show through `perm_template_notice_pages`, so a person who no longer looks after the template no longer sees them. A page made by a set carries the set in its event: its watchers hear nothing of it, and the set's own event tells each watcher once with the number of its pages they may see, and nobody who may see none. A person mentioned in several pages of a published set gets one row, which lists the pages. A background set tells its creator when it is ready (TPL-1433). Rows hold ids and counts; no answer and no reason travels.

**Screens** (TPL-1001, TPL-1002, TPL-1010, TPL-1030). The lists of templates have a State column with a filter of states, a section Waiting for approval, and the warning of TPL-1468 for a condition that tests a deleted group (one batched read for the whole list). The template settings gain Who can use it. The Versions section shows each version's state and decisions, Propose, and for a waiting version Approve and Send back with the comparison against the version in use; Withdraw sits at its end. Proposers find My templates in the space settings, and only that. The editor and the settings read the newest version through `GET /templates/{id}/newest`; forms and the gallery read the version in use.

## Measurements

On generated data in the development containers, with other builders' gates running beside it (TPL-1422's real-data dump was not at hand):

| Case | Budget | Measured |
|---|---|---|
| Gallery of 100 templates, 10 with lists, p95 of 31 requests | 30 ms | 9.15 ms |
| Queries of that gallery, with and without lists | at most one more than before | 5 and 5 |
| The gallery's query, `EXPLAIN ANALYZE` | hash joins | 0.43 ms; the lists are index-only probes of `template_rights_pkey` per template, not hash joins |

The planner chose probes by key over hash joins at this size; at 110 rows that is cheaper. Whether it switches on a larger instance was not measured.

## What was not proven

- The budgets on the real-data dump (TPL-1422), and the plan of the gallery on more templates than a hundred.
- An automation rule whose owner leaves a users list: the rule goes through the same refusal, whose words name the case, but no test runs a rule (TPL-505).
- The readable change list of TPL-602; the decision screen shows the comparison of texts and "something besides the texts changed".
- Widening a template of a space to all spaces (TPL-512, TPL-1487).
- The pictures with fifty templates (TPL-1001).
- `space.audit` showing the template events of its space was not tested on its own (TPL-712); the events carry the space.
- The starter templates after the migration (TPL-531): they are made approved, with no test of their own.
- A mail rendered for each new type: the words exist, and the test checks the rows and the events, not a sent mail.

## Tests

The refusal tests were written after a first draft of the code, not before it; each was then seen to fail with its check removed, which the mutations below repeat.

- `internal/app/template_rights_test.go`: a users list only narrows (a listed person without `page.create`, an outsider on nine routes, a form submitted after its person left the list); an editors list only narrows, its lock-out, and clearing by whoever runs the space; chosen spaces and a deleted one; a waiting or withdrawn version makes no pages for anyone, the owner's try makes drafts, an approver's dry run works; the approved version stays in use, a replaced one works for a day and not after a withdrawal; no self-approval with four eyes on, direct publishing with them off, and who may set them; a proposer neither approves, publishes, hides, withdraws nor makes a template of all spaces; two approvers at once, a changed proposal; approval checks the template again; who sees each state, and a draft of an owner who left; approval notifications and audit; a withdrawal during a set; only the newest version is proposed; every new route off while the part is off; lists, drafts and four eyes held while an older release runs.
- `internal/app/template_notify_test.go`: one notification per published set with partial access, none for a watcher who sees nothing, none for drafts; one mention for a set of three pages; no answer in any notification or event; the gallery's query count with and without lists; the load budget and plan of the gallery (run when asked).
- `internal/exchange/grants_test.go`: a version 8 bundle brings `template.propose` with `page.create`.
- The permission matrix and the adoption test of wave PM cover `template.propose` through `SplitFrom`.
- `web/e2e/templates-form.spec.ts`: propose, send back with a reason, approve and withdraw, by keyboard.
- `web/e2e/0-design.spec.ts`: pictures `templates-states`, `template-access`, `template-decision`, `template-send-back` and `my-templates`.
