# Wave PB4: draft and published spaces, existing spaces and the last steps

Builder report for spec 073, wave PB4, the last wave of the part. It covers what was built, what was measured, what was decided on the product owner's behalf, and what is not proven yet. The part stays experimental and off by default.

## What was built

**Starting a draft space from a space with content** (PUB-1302, PUB-1309 to PUB-1311). In the settings section Published space, a space in no pair that holds content offers Start a draft space from this space, beside Link a published space. Its preview says, in numbers: the pages, folders and links that are copied and paired, how many of them the person cannot see and how many carry a view restriction, the files and their size, who edits the space now and will edit the draft space instead, the blog posts and databases that stay and are no longer edited, the archived and never-published pages that stay as they are, and about how long it takes. Confirming queues the start; the worker carries it out in one transaction that holds the rows of every page of the old space, so what is copied is what readers have, and a publish meanwhile waits and then meets the rule of a published space. The start makes the draft space, gives it every grant of the old space whose subject writes (Create pages, Edit pages, what was split off them, or Run the space) and none that only views, copies each page with its files, labels, look and restrictions, leads every link, card, block, file and space key of the copies to the copies, pairs each copy with its source in step, and makes the old space the published space. The old pages lose their task rows, as twins keep none. The start is recorded as a run of scope `start`, which tells nobody, puts nothing in the feed and shows in the history; its pages keep the map verification needs. A space with pages under a workflow is refused, as are a personal space, a space in a pair and a key in use. Until the first release, Undo the start ends the pair and sends the draft space to the trash; it stays open while the part is off, as ending does.

**The space-wide verification** (PUB-920). Verify every published page, in the settings of a draft space for those who see its audit, checks every twin in the background, as one is checked, and reports per page: the same as its draft version, differs, draft version changed, cannot be verified yet, or draft purged. A page paired by a start is verified by doing the start again from its map, from the page readers have to its copy. The report is the result of the job, for whoever asked, and pages they cannot see are counted, not listed.

**The weekly check and the health page** (PUB-343, PUB-1305). The worker asks every hour whether the consistency check is due: a week after the last one, or once after the part was turned on again, which the experimental switches now note for each part. It only looks. System health has the line Published spaces with what it found, and administrators press Check now, or Fix what it found to recompute every status. Turning the part off and on keeps every pair and its statuses as stored.

**What PB2 and PB3 left.** The release status of a draft page names, for those who release, what each released version became, and the history of the page says it in words (PUB-911, PUB-1223). The tree of a draft space offers Changed since release, listing those pages flat, the longest waiting first, to writers and releasers (PUB-1224). A twin tells someone who would otherwise edit it why they cannot, with the draft space's name only when they may see it (PUB-1231). Deleting either space of a pair tells the people who run the other space, by the new notification type `pair_ended` (PUB-1046, PUB-1047). A bundle of a draft space carries the left-out marks, never the pair; a bundle of a published space imports as an ordinary space (PUB-1042, PUB-1043). The release screen shows a run of approval or withdrawal that waits, goes or stopped, and it is cancelled or resumed like a larger run. A PDF, Word or other file of a published page of a controlled page carries the control block of its approval: Approved, the version readers cite, the date, the approver only when the pair shows approvers, and the signatures of the version released, the part of PUB-731 that DC4 left to this wave (DC4-21). The restore drill makes a pair and a run going at the backup, and proves that after the restore the pair is back, the twin still refuses a write by hand, and the run shows as stopped and is resumed to the end (PUB-1032).

**Guides.** `docs/operations/published-spaces.md`, the operator guide of the part, and a section of `docs/operations/upgrade.md` on what an upgrade must take care of once pairs exist (PUB-1312).

**Data.** Migration 0072 widens the check of `release_runs.scope` with `start`. Expand only; it may run twice. Space events in the outbox now carry the actor's id.

## Measurements

Measured in the development toolbox on generated data, with other builders' gates running at the same time (load average about 20). The real-data dump of wave PF was not on this machine, so PUB-1427 and PUB-1108 are not measured, as in PB2 and PB3.

| What | Budget | Measured |
|---|---|---|
| Preview of a start of 1,004 pages | 2 s, as a preview of a run (PUB-1104) | 120 ms; it estimates 30 s |
| Start of 1,004 pages, one transaction | background work, at least 50 pages a second as a run (PUB-1105) | 27 s, 37 pages a second |
| Space-wide verification of 1,004 twins | background work (PUB-920) | 2.7 s, every twin verified |
| Whole-space release of 2,001 pages, 9 batches, measured again | 50 pages a second (PUB-1105) | 73 s, 27 pages a second |
| Whole-space release of 981 pages, one transaction, measured again | 30 s | 26 s, 37 pages a second |
| Release screen at 5,001 pages over HTTP, measured again | 100 ms (PUB-1103) | 74 ms best, 97 ms first |

A start costs about what a release of the same pages costs here: each page is a page row, its files, restrictions and labels, a version, and its pair, written by the same code as any copy. Editing the old space waits for the length of the start (D52), so the preview names it. The note for would-be editors adds one permission check to the status of a twin, which the page asks for beside its read; the read of a twin itself is unchanged (PUB-1100). The weekly check costs what `diffluence check publishing` costs, once a week.

## Upgrade impact

Migration 0072 widens the check of `release_runs.scope` to take `start`; expand only, and it may run twice. The previous release reads a start as a run whose scope it does not name. Starting a draft space, the space-wide verification and the weekly check are work of the worker of this release: finish upgrading every node that runs the worker before using them. Space events now carry the actor's id, which the previous release ignores. The health page has one more line, which says the part is off until it is turned on. Nothing else changes until an administrator turns the part on.

## Decided on the product owner's behalf on 2026-11-15

D52 to D71 in the decision table of spec 073, each "decided on the product owner's behalf on 2026-11-15, may be overruled": the start as one transaction in the background that holds the old space's pages; what a start copies and what stays; no start of a space under a workflow; which grants move; pages hidden from the starter copied with their restrictions; the copy changing ids only, kept as a run of its own scope and verified in its own direction; task rows of twins; old page pairs of a twin taken over; undo as its own action; the report of the space-wide verification as a job's result; when the check runs and the health line; released as version N in the status; what the tree view lists; who gets the note; who is told when a deleted space ends a pair; the bundle field; the drill step; resuming approval runs; the control block of a published copy's file; and what is left for later. The spec has no open question left for this wave.

## Not proven, and not built

- **Real data and the server.** Every number above is of generated data on a loaded development machine; PUB-1108 and PUB-1427 wait for the real-data dump and the server's next stress test. A start of more than about 1,000 pages was not measured; it holds the rows of every page of the old space for its whole length (D52).
- **Not built:** PUB-1033 (no restore of one space exists); a count of pages done while a start runs (D52); the 50,000-page limit of a start is the limit of a run and was not reached in a test.
- **Not tested on their own:** a publish in the old space that waits for a start and then meets the rule of a published space; the notification of a deleted space by mail and in the digest; restoring a draft space that undoing a start sent to the trash (the pair does not come back, D60, PUB-1034); the space-wide verification of pages hidden from the person, which are counted and not listed; the cost of the permission check that the note for would-be editors adds to the status of a twin.
- **Mutations** ran for the folders of the files they change and the changed test packages, not for every changed path; the web changes are covered by the web checks and e2e, not by mutations of their own.
- **The picture `publishing-start`** was made while the preview still said "1 seconds"; the words now say "1 second", a difference below the threshold of the picture test.
