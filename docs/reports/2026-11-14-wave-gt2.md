# Wave GT2: guided templates, fields, the form and the check step

Builder report for spec 074, wave GT2. The study is `docs/blueprint/09-enterprise/templates/`, chapters 04, 05, 11, 12 and 13; the decisions taken while building are TPL-1441 to TPL-1462 in the spec's decision table.

## What was built

**The kinds of field** (`internal/templates/fields`, TPL-300 to TPL-325). Line, text and choice, as before, and number, date, yes/no, page, person, people, choices, link and label. A field's `Spec` holds what it asks beyond its name: required, help (300 characters), an example in the field (100), options (50 of 200), lengths, a named form for a line (email address, whole number, letters and digits), lowest, highest and decimals (0 to 4), earliest and latest date (fixed, or days or weeks from the day the set is made), how many to choose, a space for a page, how yes/no and choices show, and a suggested answer. `Spec.Check` refuses a field its author got wrong, such as a required yes/no field or a suggestion that is no option any more; `Answer` checks one answer and gives it as it lands; `Fill` gives what it puts in the page. All three are pure.

**One table for Go and TypeScript** (TPL-325). `internal/templates/fields/testdata/cases.json` holds 106 answer cases, 50 field cases and 8 suggestion cases: empty values, bounds on both sides, comma and point, a thousands sign, too many decimals, month ends, a leap day in a leap year and in another, relative limits across a year end, other link schemes, people over 20, and more. `fields_test.go` and `web/src/template-fields.test.ts` both run it. A further test reads the problem codes from the Go source and fails when the form has no words for one.

**Where the settings live** (TPL-1441, TPL-1442). The manifest of a version gains `fields`, holding only what a question's text does not already say. The field in the text keeps its name and `line`, `text` or `choice` as kind, so the previous release reads every new kind as a line. Saving settings equal to the text keeps a template plain, so it stays offered while guided templates are off; any setting beyond the text makes it guided. A save is refused, with every problem named, when a setting names a question no text asks, a reserved name, a setting the kind lacks, or a suggestion that no longer fits, including a suggested person who left (TPL-316).

**The server checks every answer** (`internal/templates/answers.go`, TPL-320). `prepare`, which the dry run and the real run share, now checks the answers of the version the form is bound to: the rules of the table, then what only the database knows. A page must be one the creator may view and not in the trash, and of the field's space when it names one; otherwise it is "not found", and the answer never holds its title. A person must be one the access rule lets the creator see, which includes a guest of a shared space, and active. A label must be on the space's fixed list when it has one. A question left out takes its suggestion. Every refused answer comes back at once as `fields` in the error, each with the field, a code, its values and English words (`apperr.FieldProblem`, TPL-324). A one-line answer with a line break and a choice that is no option are refused now through the API as well (TPL-1457, TPL-302).

**What lands in the page** (TPL-212). A date mark, a mention, mentions with commas, a page link, text with hard breaks, or paragraphs when a text answer stands alone in its paragraph; choices with commas or as a list; yes/no as "Yes" or "No", or nothing. A title shows names and page titles, never ids.

**The form** (`web/src/components/template-form.tsx`, TPL-1020 to TPL-1026). In the gallery's `Dialog`, one step at a time with "Step 2 of 4" in a status line. Each question is a `Field` with "(required)" in words, help and error tied to the input with `aria-describedby`, and `aria-invalid`. Up to six options are radio buttons, more a list; choices are checkboxes; a person uses `SubjectPicker`; a page the page picker, narrowed to the space where the field names one; a number is text with a decimal keyboard; a date the browser's own date input. An error shows once a field is left or Next is pressed, never while a person first types in an empty field, and goes as soon as the answer is right. Next or Create with errors moves the focus to a summary that links to each field. Back keeps every answer; Escape and the close button ask first when anything was typed (`ConfirmDialog`, through a new `onEscape` on `Dialog`). The last step holds the place of the pages and their labels (TPL-333, TPL-1444). A guided template then shows the check step, from the dry run (TPL-340, TPL-341): the answers as a description list with a Change link each, the pages indented as they will sit with their labels as `#` words, a lock and words for a restriction, a note on titles that exist, and whether they will be drafts. The button says "Create 7 drafts" or "Create 7 pages". A refusal of the server sends the form back to the first step with a refused answer, with the words beside the field and every answer kept.

**The template's settings** (`web/src/views/template-settings.tsx`, TPL-1010 to TPL-1012). Opening a template from the list opens the Settings frame at `/templates/{id}/settings?section=`, with General (name, description, category, labels, title pattern, hidden, promoted, publish at once, and the usual place of a space template, ORG-051), Pages (the list of GT1, now with Move up, Move down, Indent and Outdent and a sentence "This template makes up to 7 pages, 3 levels deep", through `POST /templates/{id}/entries/{key}/move`), Form (steps as rows with Move up and Move down, each question's step, kind and required, and its settings in a `Dialog` with only the settings its kind has, the validator's words next to the setting they concern, and Try the form, which ends in a dry run and makes nothing) and Versions (each version with its author and page count, and what it changed page by page in the comparison pages use, ORG-060). On a phone it says it is easier on a larger screen and hides nothing (TPL-1042). `/` in a guided template's text offers a value of the template or a link to a page of its set (TPL-1013).

**The panel beside the editor** (ORG-045, `web/src/components/template-panel.tsx`). An empty page shows a panel with a search box, a few templates and the whole gallery. It stands beside the sheet where the editor has room, and above it elsewhere; it goes when typing starts.

**Not experimental** (TPL-1104): the steps, the place on the last step, the usual place, the Versions section and its routes (which leave the switch, TPL-1451), and the panel. Everything else is part of `guided_templates`; `PUT /templates/{id}/fields` and the move route answer `experimental_off` while it is off, as the test of every route proves.

## Measurements

Generated data in the development containers on a machine loaded by other builders' mutation runs (load average 43 to 46 on the WSL side), outside the race detector: `DIFFLUENCE_LOAD_BUDGET=1 go test ./internal/app/ -run 'TestTheLoadBudgetOfTheDryRun|TestTheLoadBudgetOfASet|TestASinglePageFromATemplateCostsWhatItDidBefore' -v`. The real-data dump of wave PF that TPL-1422 asks for was not at hand, so these show the order of size, not the claim.

| Budget | Spec | Measured |
|---|---|---|
| The dry run of 10 pages, p95 of 40 | 150 ms (TPL-904) | 27 ms; median 17 ms |
| The dry run of 100 pages, p95 of 40 | 1 s (TPL-904) | 34 ms; median 24 ms |
| A set of 10 pages, p95 of 40 | 300 ms (TPL-901) | 118 ms; median 97 ms (GT1: 124 to 136 ms) |
| A set of 100 pages as background work, p95 of 7 | 3 s (TPL-901) | 1.03 s; median 0.95 s (GT1: 1.00 to 1.15 s) |
| Sealing the audit events of a 100-page set | 5 s with the one-second tick (TPL-903) | at most 0.36 s |
| One page from a template | within 10% of before (TPL-901) | median 19.4 ms, slowest 27.6 ms (GT1 measured medians of 16.8 to 20.4 ms on the same machine) |

Each page of the dry-run template asks a line, a number, a date, choices, a person and a page, so the measurement includes the lookups of a person and a page. The dry run does not grow much with the pages, because it reads all texts in one query and checks each answer once, whatever the number of pages that use it. Checking the answers adds one query for a page answer and two per person; a form without such questions adds none.

## What was not proven

- The budgets on the real-data dump (TPL-1422).
- The dates of the form in a second locale (TPL-1043). The form writes dates with `Intl.DateTimeFormat` in the reader's language, and the server's words write them out in English; only English exists today.
- The text of a page other than the first in a Focus frame of its own (TPL-1011, TPL-1454): it is still written in place under the Pages list.
- Rules and Who can use it (GT3, GT4), conditions on steps and fields, and the live region that announces a question a condition shows (TPL-1023).
- Renaming a question across entries and conditions (TPL-230).
- Export and import of a template keep no settings of its questions yet (TPL-1106, GT5).
- The bell for a background set (TPL-1433, GT4).
- The upgrade from GT1 with the part already on: GT2 adds no migration, so nothing refuses a node of GT1 beside GT2; the changelog asks to finish the upgrade first (TPL-1461).
