# Wave CS1: the console shows what runs the installation

Builder report for wave CS1 of spec 075 (study: blueprint 11). It covers what was built and proven in this wave.

## What was built

**Upgrades** (`internal/app/installation.go`, `web/src/views/admin-installation.tsx`). `GET /api/v1/admin/upgrades` reads the node registry (now with each node's log level), `SHOW server_version`, `db.Status` and `db.PendingContract`, and the newest `system.upgrade_started` through the new `audit.LatestOf`. Each waiting contract migration names the live nodes that do not know it yet and when its grace ends: the youngest live start plus `CONTRACT_GRACE`, as `contractDue` decides. The grace shows with the source it was started with. "How to upgrade" shows the commands of Compose, Helm and the packages (D29).

**Events.** `GET /api/v1/admin/events` gives what `diffluence events status` prints: `events.ReadState` and `events.NATSRemovalProblem`. Read-only; the switch stays a command (CS4).

**Audit.** `audit.ReadOverview` lists the newest checkpoint and the keys, the installation's own first, each with whether the installation endorsed it (a key without its endorsement is shown and marked, not hidden), and only the first 16 hex characters of a public key. `audit_witness_checks` (migration 0065) holds reports; the application role may add and read, never change or remove (`db.GrantApp`). `diffluence audit verify --witness FILE --report` records what the comparison found, also when it fails, and refuses `--report` without `--witness`. A chain broken in itself is reported as a failed comparison at its position.

**Health.** `health.Facts` gains `Thresholds`, `BackupInterval` and the witness facts. The disk rules use the thresholds in force; the backup line warns after the interval plus two hours, or 26 hours without a schedule (`backups.Service.Interval`: the console's schedule, else the worker's default); the audit line is a problem when the newest witness report failed, and a warning when it is older than eight days once one was ever reported. The disk band names the refuse level in force.

**The log level for a while** (`internal/settings/runtime.go`, `internal/app/runtime.go`). The key `log_override` holds a level, a node or every node, and an end. Every node rereads it with the section `health` every five seconds (`settings.ReadRuntime`, one statement), sets its `slog.LevelVar` (`app.ControlLogLevel`, given by `cmdServe`), and reports its level in the heartbeat's existing upsert. When the end passes, the configured level returns without anyone asking. `PUT`/`DELETE /api/v1/admin/log-level` are an administrator's; a level quieter than the answering node's configured one is refused with `confirmation_required` and goes through `PUT /api/v1/admin/log-level/confirmed`, a system administrator with a fresh login and a second factor (D26). The second-factor check of the confirmed routes is one helper now, `confirmSecondFactor`.

**Disk thresholds.** Section `health`: warn 5 to 50, refuse 1 to 10, refuse always below warn, zero for the defaults. Lower levels are a confirmed change (D27); `health` joined `confirmedSections`. The storage guard reads the refuse level at every check (`FollowRefuseLevel`) on its cached reading of the room, so a new level holds within the five-second reread.

**Monitoring files.** `internal/monitoring` embeds copies of `deploy/monitoring/`; a test keeps them equal. `GET /api/v1/admin/monitoring` says whether `/metrics` is on; `GET /api/v1/admin/monitoring/{kind}` sends `rules` or `dashboard` as a download.

**Configuration.** The process keeps the list it read at its start (`app.startedWith`); the page marks a value or source that differs (`config.CompareWithStart`) and shows the running value. `config.TracingVariables` lists `OTEL_EXPORTER_OTLP_*`, `OTEL_SERVICE_NAME` and `OTEL_RESOURCE_ATTRIBUTES`: headers, keys and resource attributes as `(set)`, endpoints without user, password, query and fragment (D30). `config.ProbeRecord` keeps the last try of the outside settings, which `cmdServe` makes at the start; the page shows it and never tries again. The settings judged for security carry a sentence why they are not changed here (D31). No route writes the configuration.

**Sessions.** `GET /api/v1/admin/sessions/count` gives the sessions and people that logging everyone out ends; the dialog says it before anyone confirms. The page names the incident steps.

## How the tests prove it

Permission paths first, in `internal/app/installation_console_test.go`:

| Test | Proves |
|---|---|
| `TestAMemberReachesNoAreaOfTheInstallation` | Every new read and write is 403 for a member, and nothing is recorded |
| `TestATokenReachesNoAreaOfTheInstallation` | A token of an administrator gets `session_only` on each area |
| `TestAnAdministratorCannotMakeTheInstallationQuieterOrFuller` | A quieter level and lower thresholds are refused to an administrator through both routes and nothing is stored; louder and higher stay theirs |

## Load budget (CSL-701 to CSL-705)

- **Steady, per node:** one statement every five seconds, a primary-key lookup of two keys of `settings` (D35). The study planned it inside the maintenance read; that read runs per request with a cache, so a separate background read is cheaper to reason about. The heartbeat writes the level in the upsert it already makes: no new statement.
- **Health facts:** three more small statements in the read that is cached for 30 seconds (the runtime keys, the schedule, the newest witness report by its index).
- **Pages while open:** the upgrade page about six small statements per 15-second poll (nodes, version, the migration table twice, the marker); events two; the audit area three; the log level two. Nothing on saving, reading or search.
- **Not measured** on the real-data dump of wave PF in this wave; the figures are counts of statements, not times.

## What is not proven

- The log level reaches the backup service only from CS2; the backup service's code line is printed by its own process and was not tested at level error here.
- The page timings of CSL-703 on the dump.
- Two nodes taking a log level for one of them: tested on one node with the id of another.
- A real restart showing the mark "changes at the next restart" in the browser: tested in Go only.
