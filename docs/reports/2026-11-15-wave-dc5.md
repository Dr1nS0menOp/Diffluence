# Report: wave DC5, document control: time, review, read and understood, moves, imports and migration (15 November 2026)

## What happened

Wave DC5 is the fifth and last part of spec 064. Its scope is DOC-120 to DOC-122, DOC-230 to DOC-232, DOC-240 to DOC-242, DOC-402, DOC-601, DOC-800 to DOC-805, DOC-820 to DOC-824, DOC-1000 to DOC-1002, DOC-1030 and DOC-1031, DOC-1100 to DOC-1107, DOC-1200 to DOC-1204, DOC-1210 to DOC-1213, DOC-1220, DOC-1223, DOC-1232, DOC-1240 to DOC-1242, DOC-1251, DOC-1252 and DOC-1304 to DOC-1306. The study marks each line built, partly or new; what is left is listed under "Not in this wave" in the spec. The decisions taken on the product owner's behalf are DC5-1 to DC5-17 in the spec.

## What was built

- **The model (DOC-103, DOC-105, DOC-120, DOC-230, DOC-800, DOC-820).** A review state takes a due period with a reminder and escalation targets; two new outcomes are taken by the system on time, `overdue` (never into approval) and `review_due` (from a timed approved state into review). A timed state takes a period in months, an approved state the groups that confirm reading, and a step its conditions, joined through `conditions.Combine` within `conditions.MaxTests`. The validator checks each rule, with a table test per rule.
- **Due dates and the job (DOC-230 to DOC-232, DOC-601, DOC-1232, DOC-1240 to DOC-1242, DOC-1304).** A stage with a due period gets a row in `review_deadlines`, counted by `control_working_days_after` in the database from now() of the stage, in the instance's zone and working week, a new setting under General. The job `control-due` reads due rows by their stored moment, at most 500 a pass, and acts on each in its own transaction that locks the page with SKIP LOCKED, checks the moment again and moves it on: the reminder before the date, then what the state says when it passes. The time-out step is taken by the system and recorded with its cause; a step into approval is refused before anything is written, also when a stored definition holds one.
- **Escalation, delegation, absence and leavers (DOC-231, DOC-240 to DOC-242, DOC-1220).** Escalation asks its targets once, never an author under independence. A reviewer hands their place on only to someone the same entry names; whoever looks after the workflows may choose another person who may review; never a guest, an author, someone already asked, or someone who may not sign a signed approval. An absence with a stand-in replaces an absent reviewer when a stage starts, only when the state names the stand-in, for at most three steps. The job drops disabled and removed reviewers, and a stage that can no longer pass escalates; a removal by hand escalates too where the state names a target.
- **Periodic review (DOC-800 to DOC-805, DOC-1223).** An approval into a timed state sets the next review date, shown on the band to readers; a page may shorten its period. The owner, or the space's administrators when the owner left, is reminded; on the date the state's periodic step starts a round on the same text, and its passing confirms the version without a new one.
- **Read and understood (DOC-820 to DOC-824).** An approval into a state that asks for reading resolves its groups and asks their members; the job asks people who join later. The band offers the confirmation only after the page showed that version in the same session; a token never confirms. The list is for `space.audit`, as JSON or CSV.
- **Exempt labels and conditions (DOC-120, DOC-121, DOC-402).** A space workflow exempts labels; putting one on, taking it off or renaming onto or away from one needs `space.workflows`, and a copy drops one its maker may not set. The band names why a step is held back, and the step is refused with the reason, through a token too.
- **Audit and reports (DOC-1000 to DOC-1002, DOC-1030, DOC-1031).** Each new action writes an audit entry; a step's comment is in it; `diffluence audit prune` stops at the oldest record of document control. Five reports for a space and across spaces, under `space.audit`, as JSON or CSV.
- **Moves, copies, trash and archive (DOC-1200 to DOC-1204).** A move to another space leaves its page workflow behind and drops an approval of the other space's procedure; under control there the page starts hidden from readers, without control it is an ordinary page at its approved version. A copy holds no approval or signature, and a reader's copy holds the approved text. The trash and the archive keep the state and the record.
- **Imports (DOC-1212, DOC-1213).** A bundle's workflows lose the people of the other installation; a bundle whose readers' version is not the approved one is refused; bundled signatures become `imported_signatures`, shown with their installation, verified against the content here, and approving nothing. A Markdown import into a controlled space makes pages that wait for approval.
- **Today's approvals (DOC-1100 to DOC-1107).** A space that requires approval is offered "Approval as before" with a preview of where each page lands, in the three cases of DOC-1102; accepting keeps every past approval, marked as approved before signatures; the old rule is off while the space follows the workflow, and stays readable; undo works until a person first acts under it.
- **Screens (DOC-924).** The band shows the due date, the next review date, an approval carried over, the reading request with its button, and why a step is held back. The review panel shows the due date, how each reviewer came to be asked, and hands a review on. The workflow editor sets due periods, the time outcomes and periodic review; the general settings set the working week.

## Load

The budgets of spec 064 for this wave are the due job (at most 500 rounds a pass, without holding locks long) and the reports within the read limit (DOC-1304, DOC-1305). Measured on the development database while other builders' gates ran, at a load of 11 to 13:

- **The due job:** 1200 deadlines of rounds that had ended were closed in 30 ms, in passes of at most 500, each a short statement; a live deadline is its own transaction of a few small queries that locks one page.
- **Reads:** the due date, the review date and the reading request ride in the query the band already makes; `TestAControlledReadCostsAtMostOneQueryMore` still holds. A workflow with conditions costs its authors three to five small queries on the band.
- **Not measured:** the real-data dump of wave PF was not loaded, so the budgets of DOC-1306 and the reports on real data are not re-measured; nor the due job with 10,000 live rounds.

## Checks

- **Tests first, in part.** The refusal tests of delegation, absence, people who leave, moves, imports, exempt labels and conditions were written before the code and seen failing on their first run; one of them found that a move to another space kept the approval and the page workflow of the old space. The refusal tests of time, reading and periodic review were written before their code too, but a panic in an earlier test stopped that first run before them, so their failing was shown afterwards by the mutation check instead: removing each guard makes them fail. The tests of copies, the trash and the archive passed at once: that behaviour held already.
- **`make check`:** passed on main `9c4c1a0` with this wave on top, with `TEST_SHARDS=2`.
- **Targeted mutation check:** `MUTATION_JOBS=2` with every changed path outside `internal/app` and the script: 477 of 1267 ran. Nine were not caught at first: six DC5 mutations did not compile, "a reviewer is taken off with too few left" of DC2 no longer applied to the changed removal, and two were not seen by their tests. Each was repaired or its test made stronger (a chain of stand-ins that needs four steps; pruning that keeps nothing), one guard that no test could reach was removed with its mutation, and the nine were then run again, the same way, and caught. 24 mutations are new.
- **Boundary:** six new tables belong to pages; the reads of `space_grants` and `audit_events` and the further reads of `users`, `group_members`, `spaces` and `attachments` are listed with their reasons.
- **E2E:** `control.spec.ts` and `0-design.spec.ts` on the rebased tree, 128 passed, with a new test of the review date and the reading confirmation. Three pictures changed and were looked at: `review-panel` and `review-panel-phone-rtl` gain "Hand on" for whoever looks after the workflows, and `admin-rules` gains the working week.
- **Web:** tsc, eslint, prettier, strings, design and vitest pass.

## Not proven in this wave

- A full restore with an open round that escalates (DOC-1211), a large assignment killed and resumed (DOC-1251), and a failing mail sender (DOC-1252): not run in this wave.
- The budgets of DOC-1306 on the real-data dump.
- A clock that really moves: the test moves the stored moments instead, which is what the job sees, and the due moment is counted from the database's time by a function of the database.
- The PDF report and the background export of the auditor's export, and a signed reading confirmation: not built.
