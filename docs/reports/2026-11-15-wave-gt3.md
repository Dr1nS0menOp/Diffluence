# Wave GT3: guided templates, conditions

Builder report for spec 074, wave GT3. The study is `docs/blueprint/09-enterprise/templates/`, chapter 06 with chapters 04, 05, 11 and 12; the decisions taken while building are TPL-1463 to TPL-1476 in the spec's decision table.

## What was built

**One small package for conditions** (`internal/conditions`, TPL-400 to TPL-403, TPL-430). A condition is "all of" or "any of" at most ten tests on one level. A test has a subject (a question, the space, or the groups of the creator), an operator from the table of its kind, and a value, a list of values, or "the day the pages are made". `Validate` refuses what an author got wrong against what the condition may test where it stands; `CheckShape` refuses what no template could make valid, for a section of text, where the questions are not known; `Holds` decides, never fails, and treats every malformed condition as false. `Combine` and `MaxTests` are the shared part document control's transitions reuse (TPL-433): DC5 comes second and should build on them. The package is pure and imports nothing of Diffluence.

**One table for Go and TypeScript** (TPL-401, TPL-403, TPL-431). `internal/conditions/testdata/cases.json` holds the operators of every kind, 130 cases of single tests (each operator of each kind on an empty answer, and bounds on both sides), 16 combined conditions (all, any, space, groups, a deleted group, malformed ones), 30 validator cases and 8 sentences. `conditions_test.go` and `web/src/template-conditions.test.ts` both run it; the sentences are checked by TypeScript, which writes them. A test reads the problem codes from the Go source and fails when the editor has no words for one.

**What a condition switches** (TPL-420 to TPL-424):
- a question (in its settings, `condition`) and a step (`condition` on the step), testing only questions the form asks before them, so no cycle can be saved; the order is the form's own, step by step, then the questions of no step;
- a page of the set (`PUT /templates/{id}/entries/{key}/condition`): when it does not hold, the page and every page below it are not made, and a link to it becomes the title it would have had, as plain text; the first page cannot have one;
- a section of text, the new block `conditional`, allowed only in templates and up to three deep: unwrapped when it holds, removed with everything in it when it does not; a file only removed sections showed is not copied;
- label rules (`PUT /templates/{id}/rules`): a fixed label or the label chosen in a label question, always or when a condition holds; a label off a space's fixed list is left out and reported.

**The server decides** (TPL-421, TPL-431, decided TPL-1406, TPL-1407). `checkAnswers` walks the form in its order: a step or question whose condition does not hold is hidden, its answer is dropped unread, it is not required, and it is empty everywhere it is used. The same facts then decide pages, sections and rules, in the dry run and the real run alike. The creator's groups are read only for a template that tests them, as the permission rule resolves them; a group that is gone makes its test false. Rights are checked for the pages the set makes (TPL-1466).

**Checked at save** (TPL-432). `saveVersion` runs `checkConditions` after the settings of questions: every condition of every place, with each problem named at once as `invalid_conditions`, the place (`Badge`, `step:2`, `entry:<key>`, `section:<key>`, `rule:3`), a code and the test. A text change that removes a question a condition still tests is refused (TPL-1469).

**Renaming a question** (TPL-230, `POST /templates/{id}/fields/rename`): in every text that asks it, every section, the title patterns, its settings, its step, every condition and every rule, as one new version.

**The editor** (TPL-1010, TPL-1011, TPL-1013, TPL-1014). A condition is edited in a `Dialog` as native fields: "Applies when" all or any, then rows of what is tested, how and the value, each the input its kind needs (a list of options, checkboxes for "is one of", a date or "the day the pages are made", the group picker), Add a test and Remove on each row, and the sentence above ("Only when Works on site is no"). The validator's words stand under the test they concern once Keep is pressed. The Form section has a Condition column for questions and steps, and Rename on each question; the Pages section a Condition column; the new Rules section lists the label rules. In the text, `/` offers "Section shown when…", which shows as a quiet outline in `--color-guide` with its sentence on the first line, and its block menu changes the condition or turns it back into text. A test of the groups says "This changes the text people get. It does not stop anyone reading the template" (TPL-402).

**The form** (TPL-1023). Questions and steps follow their conditions while the person types, previewed by the shared table; a question a condition shows appears after the one that caused it, "1 more question" (or "1 question fewer") is said in a polite live region, and the focus never moves by itself. Answers to hidden questions are not sent.

## Measurements

The budget of TPL-905, evaluating every condition of a 100-entry template within 1 ms, in a Go benchmark (`TestEveryConditionOfAHundredEntryTemplateTakesUnderAMillisecond`, not under the race detector): 249 conditions (99 entries, 40 questions, 10 steps, 100 sections), each of 10 tests going round every operator, with the facts read once per set.

| Case | Spec | Measured |
|---|---|---|
| Every condition, mixed operators | 1 ms | 0.74 to 0.84 ms, 531 allocations |
| Every test "includes all of" 50 options on an answer of 50 | none (an upper bound) | 2.4 to 3.4 ms |

The machine was loaded by other builders' gates (load average 19 to 31 in WSL), so these are an upper estimate; the first version of the evaluator took 20 ms on the costliest case and 1 ms on the mixed one, before it read each answer once per set instead of once per test. The fuzz target (`FuzzEvaluationIsTotalAndBounded`) ran 1.8 million inputs in 20 s without a panic or a run over its fixed bound of steps. The dry run and the set budgets of GT1 and GT2 were not measured again: conditions add no query unless the groups of the creator are tested, which adds two.

## What was not proven

- The budgets on the real-data dump (TPL-1422).
- The warning in the template list for a deleted group (TPL-402, TPL-1468): a save is refused and the test is false, but the list does not say so yet.
- Words compared ignoring capitals use the lower-case of each language's runtime; for a few letters (such as the dotted capital I) Go and the browser differ, so the preview could differ from the server's decision there. The server decides.
- The upgrade from GT2 with the part on: GT3 adds no migration, so nothing refuses a node of GT2 beside GT3, which would drop conditions and sections when it saves a template (TPL-1476); the changelog asks to finish the upgrade first.
- A screen-reader run by a person; the live region is tested by its text and role only.

## Tests

Written before the code they prove: the shared table and the Go and TypeScript suites against stubs, and the integration tests against the server of GT2, where each failed for the reason it names before the code was written.

- `internal/conditions`: the shared table, the operators of each kind, the words of every problem, `Combine`, the fuzz target, and the budget.
- `internal/document/conditional_test.go`: the shape of a section's condition (no condition, words, no test, eleven, a group inside a test, an unknown key, an expression, an unknown subject), three deep and no deeper (also through a panel), no publishing, resolving true, false and nested sections, an emptied block keeping a paragraph, and a rename.
- `internal/app/guided_conditions_test.go`, failure paths first: a tampered submit answering a hidden question, also with a refused value, on both the dry and the real route; a hidden step; a condition on a question that does not exist, on each place, and a text change removing a tested question; a self-reference, a cycle and a step testing its own question; the validator (ten and eleven tests, nesting, a wrong operator, an expression as a value, a missing option, a deleted group, the space in a space template, no match, the first page); an entry left out with its children and a link to it; sections true, false and nested with their files, and a draft and a published page holding a section; label rules and a fixed list; a member, a non-member and a deleted group; a rename across three entries, a step, a rule and conditions, with its refusals.
- `internal/app/guided_templates_test.go`: the three new routes answer `experimental_off` while the part is off.
- `web/e2e/templates-form.spec.ts`: a question a condition shows, by keyboard, announced, the focus kept, and a hidden answer not sent; the condition editor by keyboard with its sentence.
- `web/e2e/0-design.spec.ts`: pictures of the Form section with conditions, the condition dialog, the Pages section with conditions, the Rules section, a section in the editor, and the form with a shown question at 360 px and right to left.
