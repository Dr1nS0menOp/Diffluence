# Wave GT5: guided templates, history, origin, search, export, automation and restore

Builder report for spec 074, wave GT5, the last wave of guided templates. The study is `docs/blueprint/09-enterprise/templates/`, chapters 08, 09, 10, 11, 12 and 13; the decisions taken while building are TPL-1498 to TPL-1516 in the spec's decision table. GT5 also takes what GT1 to GT4 left for it: the readable change list (TPL-602), export and import keeping the settings of questions, template events in the space audit (TPL-712), the starters after the migration (TPL-531), a rule whose owner leaves a users list (TPL-505) and the pictures with fifty templates (TPL-1001). The bell for a background set was built in GT4 (TPL-1433).

## What was built

**The change list** (TPL-602, `internal/templates/changes.go`). A pure function compares two manifests with their texts and gives one line per change besides the texts: pages added, removed, renamed, moved and reordered; their title patterns, labels, restrictions, workflows and conditions; questions added (with kind and whether required), removed, of another kind, made required or not, with other settings or another condition; steps added, removed, asking other questions, with another condition, or reordered; label rules; publishing at once; the usual place. Each line is a code with the names the author typed, so the browser words it and the audit keeps it: `template.version_saved` now holds the list, at most 50 lines. The comparison route gives it as `list`, beside the texts.

**History and sets** (TPL-613, `origin.go`). The history counts the sets of each version for those who manage the template, and `GET /templates/{id}/versions/{number}/sets` lists the 100 newest whose first page the manager may see; the rest is a number. The Versions section compares a version with any earlier one, and shows the count with a dialog of the sets.

**Origin** (TPL-611, TPL-612, TPL-1031). `GET /pages/{id}/template-origin` says what a page was made from, for whoever may see the page. A reader who may see the template gets its name and version, one who looks after it a link to its history, and one who may also edit the page "a newer version is in use"; others read "a template". The other pages of the set are listed through `pages.RefsOf`, so a page the reader may not see never shows. The Details panel shows it as one line.

**Search** (TPL-700 to TPL-703). The gallery searches on the server with `?q=`, by name, description, category and the names of the pages of the approved version, as one more condition of the gallery's own query, so it cannot find a template outside its users list; the browser uses it above 100 templates. `template:"Name"` in search finds the pages made from the templates of that name the reader may see, through the function `template_origin_pages` of migration 0074, which the templates module owns; every result still passes `perm_visible_pages`. A name that stands for nothing finds nothing. While the part is off, `template:` reads as the words it was before.

**Export and import** (TPL-852, TPL-1106, `bundle.go`). A file has format 2: the version in use with every page and text, the questions without a suggested person or page or the space of a page question, the steps, conditions, label rules and publishing at once, and the groups of restrictions by id and name with the number of people left out. It holds no users or editors list, chosen spaces, state, history, owner, reviewer, person, workflow or usual place. An import while the part is on is a draft of the importer, whoever imports it: people are left out of restrictions, a list they leave empty holds the importer, a group that is not here stays and matches nobody, and such a page is marked `choose_again`. No version is approved, at once or by a decision, while a mark stands or a restriction names someone gone; the mark goes only when the lists change, never by sending it back. A draft may keep a test of a group that is gone (TPL-1504). The import answers with `import_notes`, which the list shows. A file without a format imports as one page; a file of an unknown format, or a format-less file with parts of format 2, is refused whole; the previous release refuses a format-2 file because it reads no field `format`. While the part is off a plain file imports as before, and a guided one is refused with `experimental_off`.

**Automation** (TPL-505, TPL-604, TPL-1105, `rule.go`). The action "new page from a template" calls `UseForRule`: the owner's rights, the version approved when the rule runs, and a refusal with a code the rule's history keeps: `template_not_offered`, `template_not_approved` or `template_asks` (a required question with no suggestion that is asked whatever was answered). The rule's test run gives the same as its note.

**Restore and switching** (TPL-854, TPL-1107). The restore drill gains a step: a template in use and one withdrawn before the backup, and a background set queued after it; after the restore the first is approved at version 2, there is no set and no job, and the withdrawn one refuses to make pages. A test shows a waiting background set whose version is withdrawn making nothing when it runs. Turning the part off keeps every version, approval, entry page, origin and set, and on again brings the gallery back.

**Smaller parts.** The approval note says that the words are the approver's to judge (TPL-844). A list without templates says so. The space audit shows the template events of its space and not those of templates of all spaces (TPL-712). The starters are approved at version 1 and offered with the part on and off (TPL-531). The guide `docs/operations/templates.md` covers turning the part on, who may do what, the four-eyes setting and what approval checks.

## What was not proven

- The burst of TPL-907 on the server, and every budget on the real-data dump (TPL-1422).
- The plan of `template_origin_pages` on a large instance: it reads `template_sets` by template and the origins by set, through their indexes, but was only run on generated data.
- An automation rule in the browser: the rule editor does not offer the action, so the warning was tested through the API's test run only.
- A file exported by a node of GT4 during an upgrade carries no format and imports as one page; a guided template exported that way loses its other pages. The format number exists from this release on.

- `internal/templates/changes_test.go`: the change list for every kind of change, an unchanged version, and the words a line keeps.
- `internal/search/template_filter_test.go`: `template:` as a filter only with the part on, and as before without it.
- `internal/app/guided_templates_load_test.go`: the load of the gallery search, `template:` and a burst, run when asked.
- `web/e2e/0-design.spec.ts`: pictures `templates-none`, `template-change-list`, `template-version-sets`, `page-origin`, `template-import-note` and `templates-fifty`, in a space of their own that the test deletes.
