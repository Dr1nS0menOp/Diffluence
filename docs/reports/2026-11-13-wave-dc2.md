## What was built

- **The model.** `internal/workflow` gives a state of kind in review a review block: who reviews (people, groups and roles), the rule to pass (all, a number of them, or any one), independence, and whether authors may add reviewers. A step out of a review state may carry an outcome, approved or changes. The system takes those steps, and nobody takes them by hand. The validator checks each rule and names the state or step concerned. `Diff` names a change of reviewers as `review_changed`. Two examples ship, "Review then approve" and "Two-step review", and both pass the validator.
- **Migration 0058:**
  - the stage of a round;
  - `workflow_reviewers`: who was asked, how, their answer, and a removal with its reason;
  - `workflow_review_comments`;
  - `workflow_templates`, and the template and version of a workflow;
  - `perm_review_pages`.

  Migrations 0056 and 0057 are the waves built beside this one, already on main.
- **Rounds.**
  - **Entering a review state.** When a page enters a review state, its reviewers are resolved once, in one query, to members who view the space. Under independence, everyone who published a version since the last approved one is left out.
  - **When a round is refused.** It is refused, and so is the step that led to it, when an entry names nobody (the message names the entry), when nobody independent is left, when it would ask more than 50 people (500 under "any one"), or when the number needed is higher than the number of reviewers.
  - **Answers.** `POST /pages/{id}/review/answer` takes approve, or changes with a comment. It locks the page and then its control row, as a publish does. The answer and the step that follows are in one transaction: a step that cannot be taken refuses the answer too. One "changes" ends the round under every rule.
  - **Adding and removing reviewers.** Authors add reviewers where the state allows it, and whoever holds `space.workflows` may always add them. Removal needs `space.workflows` or the reviewer's own wish, plus a reason. A removal that leaves too few people to pass is refused; one that leaves only approvals passes the round.
- **Reading (DOC-203).**
  - `approval.read` gains the reviewer fact, loaded for that action only. It opens the page past view restrictions and past "never approved", for members only, while the round runs and the page sits in that stage.
  - `GET /pages/{id}` serves such a reviewer the working version with `review_only` and no breadcrumbs. The tree, search, the feed and the pages around it stay closed.
  - Authors and reviewers see the working version, and the band says `reviewing` and `in_review`. Steps with an outcome are never offered.
- **The review panel.** `GET /pages/{id}/review` is for the authors and the people the round asked. It shows:
  - the rule and the count;
  - the reviewers with their answers;
  - the review comments;
  - for those who may see the working version, the approved version and the version under review, to compare.

  A former reviewer keeps the record of the round, never its text, and readers get 403.
- **Notifications.** Four new kinds: asked to review, an answer, the end of a review, and a reviewer taken off. A reviewer hears through the reading being asked gives them, and the list of notifications joins `perm_review_pages`. Authors hear only while they may edit.
- **Instance templates.** An instance administrator keeps templates under `/api/v1/admin/workflow-templates`. A space copies one, and the copy records the template and its version. A template change never moves a copy: the space compares and takes the new version by choice. Reviewers that do not exist, roles that are not space roles, and guests are refused when a workflow or template is saved.
- **The validator while editing.** `POST /api/v1/workflows/check` answers the first problem.

- **Screens.**
  - **The band.** It gives each state name an icon beside its words, so the state never rests on colour (DOC-920). It has a Review button for a reviewer, or for an author of a page in review, and the key `v` opens the panel; the shortcut is listed and can be turned off like the others (DOC-930).
  - **The review panel.** It sits in the side panel, one panel at a time. It shows the rule and the count in words, the reviewers as a table with headers, the change since the approved version, and the review comments with a field to add one. Approve and Ask for changes each open a dialog with the comment field first and focused; Escape closes it and focus returns (DOC-921, DOC-922). Adding a person and removal with a reason are offered when allowed.
  - **A review-only page.** It shows the title, a note, the band, the document and the panel.
  - **The workflow editor.** In the space settings, a form of states and transitions with Move up and Move down. A review block holds people, groups and roles, the rule, the quorum, independence and "authors may add". The validator runs on each change, its message sits beside the state or step it names, and saving stays off until it is fixed (DOC-912). Before saving, a dialog lists what changes for running rounds and renamed states (DOC-913).
  - **The drawing.** A read-only drawing in `components/drawing.tsx`, the one file the design standard allows besides icons and charts, with the transitions written out as sentences (DOC-911). On a phone, a note says the editor is easier on a larger screen, and nothing is hidden (DOC-950).
  - **New workflows.** They start empty, from a translated example, or as a copy of an instance template. A copy with a newer template version offers Compare and Take.
  - **Administration.** Content, Document control lists the instance templates with the spaces that copied them, and lets an administrator make, change and archive them (DOC-902).
  - **Notifications.** The four new kinds show in the list.

## Load

The read path pays one `EXISTS` inside the query the band already makes, on the partial index `workflow_reviewers_user`, so it costs no extra query. `approval.read` pays one query for the reviewer fact, on that action only. The list of notifications adds `perm_review_pages` to its union, an index scan of the person's own open reviews. A step into review resolves its reviewers in one query and writes them in one statement. An answer is three to five small queries after its locks. This wave did not measure on the real-data dump: the read paths of readers did not change.

## Checks

- Unit tests:
  - the review rules, as a table;
  - the count per rule, including "changes" under every rule;
  - the number needed;
  - both examples;
  - the diff of a review.
- Pure permission test: a reviewer reads through restrictions for approval.read only, and never as a guest, a former reviewer, without the space, on a template or on a page in the trash.
- Integration tests in `internal/app/review_test.go`, failure and permission paths first:
  - who answers, and nobody steps around the reviewers;
  - frozen resolution, the limit, and empty groups and roles;
  - all, a number and any one, and "changes";
  - one transaction for an answer and its step;
  - independence with one author and with several;
  - removal, and adding a reviewer;
  - a restricted page for the round only;
  - the compare base and review comments;
  - notifications;
  - templates.
- The test of the workflow model and of the reviewer rule failed first, on the missing types. The integration tests were written against the routes and failed first on two faults of their own: the resolution query, and a restriction set by someone without the right. Both were fixed.
- Mutations: 16 new ones. They cover:
  - quorum counting;
  - "changes" ending a round, in the count and in the stored tally;
  - independence, at the start and when adding;
  - an answer from someone not asked;
  - a step taken by hand;
  - removal without the right, and with too few left;
  - a reader reading the round;
  - a reviewer reading after the round;
  - approval.read only;
  - guests, in the rule, at resolution and in the workflow;
  - an author approving around the reviewers.

  One mutation of DC1 ("a reader gets the head") was moved to the rewritten line.

- **`make check`:** every step passed on main `ef029b8` with this wave on top. The steps were fmt, vet, staticcheck, govulncheck, the licences, the web checks (tsc, eslint, prettier, strings, design, vitest) and the collab tests.
  - Inside `make check`, `internal/app` under `-race` passed its 20-minute limit once, while other builders' runs held the machine at a load of about 25.
  - The race tests of every package then passed with `-timeout=60m`. `internal/app` alone took 972 s.
- **Boundary (spec 072):** `workflow_reviewers`, `workflow_review_comments` and `workflow_templates` belong to `internal/pages`. The new crossings are listed with their reason:
  - pages names groups, members, roles and role holders when it resolves reviewers;
  - permissions reads the open round and its reviewers as a fact.
- **Targeted mutation check:** `MUTATION_JOBS=2`, with `internal/workflow`, `internal/permissions` and `internal/pages` changed. All 175 ran and all were caught.
  - Four of the new mutations did not compile on the first run, because they left a variable unused. They were rewritten.
  - A mutation of spec 011, "reading a page skips the check", aimed at the line that `readerOrReviewer` now holds, and was moved there.
- **E2E:** `control.spec.ts` and `0-design.spec.ts`, 104 tests, passed against the pictures.
  - The runs found two faults of the screens, both fixed. The answer dialog did not put the focus in its comment field, because the modal takes the focus after its children mount. Typing the name of a new state or step lost the focus after one letter, because its row was keyed by an id derived from the name.
- **Pictures:**
  - New: `review-panel`, `review-panel-phone-rtl`, `workflow-editor`, `workflow-editor-phone` and `admin-document-control`.
  - Changed: thirteen administration pictures, which gain "Document control" in the Content group of the sidebar.
  - Each was looked at against `docs/design/README.md`. The panel takes the place of Details and comments, the states have words beside their icons, right to left mirrors, and the administration screen keeps the settings frame with a confirmation before archiving.

## Not proven in this wave
