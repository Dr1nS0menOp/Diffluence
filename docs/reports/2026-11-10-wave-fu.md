# Report: wave FU, 10 November 2026

Written for: the product owner and whoever builds next.

Wave FU builds four answers of 10 November (ADR-040, spec 049). It also records all of that day's answers as ADR-040.

## What changed for people

- **Edit databases** is a permission of its own. A role can now edit pages without changing databases, or the other way round. Everyone who could edit pages got it in the update, so nobody can do more or less than before.
- **Several roles in a space.** The Access screen and a space's People and roles show every role someone holds. A list adds a role beside the others, a cross takes one away, and taking the last one away still asks first. Each role opens to show the permissions it gives. Giving access to someone who already holds roles adds the new one.
- **Short names.** A combination of permissions nobody named is called, for example, "Custom combination 3 (12 permissions)". It is never cut off, keeps its number, and the count follows when its permissions change. An administrator can still name it.
- **Backups before an upgrade.** An upgrade refuses to start when no backup was noted in the last 24 hours. The refusal names both ways on: run `scripts/backup.sh`, or set `DIFFLUENCE_UPGRADE_BACKUP_MAX_AGE=off`. A new installation is never refused. The changelog says this first, because it changes what happens at the next update.

## How it is built

- **One place for the rule.** `permissions.Evaluate` asks for `database.edit` wherever `page.edit` is asked of an item of kind `database`. The 41 checks and 30 routes that ask `page.edit` needed no change, and none can forget a database. `perm_editable_pages` does the same in SQL.
- **A migration that touches only its own split.** `rbac_split_roles` gives every earlier split again, which would hand `page.move_out` to a role made after migration 0040 without it. Migration 0044 uses a new `rbac_split_roles_from`, which adds `database.edit` where `page.edit` is and nothing else.
- **Mutations moved.** Three mutations aimed at `rbac_split_off` in migrations 0040 and 0041, and one at `perm_editable_pages` in 0008, would no longer bite, because 0044 defines those functions last. The first full run caught the one in 0008 missing. All four now aim at 0044.
- **Bundles** are format 5. A bundle of format 4 or older gives `database.edit` with `page.edit`.
- **The screen** reads a mark `unnamed` from the server instead of the name's first word.

## Proof

- `TestEditingADatabaseChangesForNobodyInTheMigration`: for people with every shape of role, whether they may edit a database and a page is the same before migration 0044 (by the rule as it was, which never looked at the kind) and after it (by the rule and by the SQL list).
- `TestTheMigrationAddsOnlyDatabaseEditing`, `TestDatabasesAndPagesAreEditedApart`, `TestEditingPagesIsNotEditingDatabases`, `TestEditingDatabasesIsNotEditingPages`, `TestTheBuiltInEditorEditsDatabases`.
- The permission matrix gains the new action and two grant cases, and compares the rule with the literal rule and the SQL lists.
- `TestSomeoneHoldsSeveralRolesInASpace`, and browser tests for adding, taking away, opening and naming roles.
- `TestUnnamedRoleNamesAreShortAndTheSameInGoAndSQL`, `TestCombinationsNamedBeforeGetTheShortName`, `TestAnUnnamedRoleKeepsItsCountTrue`.
- `TestTheBackupAgeForAnUpgradeIsADayByDefaultAndOffOnlyOnPurpose`, `TestAnUpgradeWithoutARecentBackupIsRefusedByDefault`, `TestAnUpgradeWithTheBackupCheckOffGoesOn`. `scripts/upgrade-test.sh` notes a backup before the new release starts, as an operator does.

Gates: `make check` passed; `scripts/e2e` 453 passed and 7 skipped; the full mutation check caught 548 of 548, 24 of them new.

## Decided in the build

| What | Choice | Why |
|---|---|---|
| What `database.edit` covers | Everything `page.edit` allows on another item, also the title, labels and versions | A database is one item; splitting its edits would surprise |
| An age of `0` | Refused at start, like a negative age or a word other than `off` | A typing mistake must not switch a safety check off |
| `diffluence migrate` | Does not check the backup, as it does not check the queue | Spec 044 put the checks in the node that migrates on start; the command is the deliberate route by hand |
| The owner of a space | Cannot lose their last role on the Access screen | The server also keeps an administrator in every space |
| A second "give access" to someone | Adds the role to what they hold | Choosing a person twice must not take roles away |

## Not proven

- The migration on an installation with thousands of holders of `page.edit`: it is one update per role, as in specs 036, 040 and 045, and was not timed at size.
- The Access screen with hundreds of rows, each holding several roles: the list is not paged (as spec 043 noted).
- The "Add a role" list is as wide as its longest choice, so its width differs per row; a fixed width had no effect and was taken out.
- A screen reader on the opening roles: they are native `details` and `summary` elements, and the automatic accessibility check passes, but no screen reader was used.
