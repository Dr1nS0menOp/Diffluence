# Wave PM: blog posts and databases apart from pages

Builder report of 2026-11-09. Spec 045, migration 0041, ADR-038.

## What was built

- **Two permissions** split off `page.create`: `post.create`, "Create blog posts" (PRM-206), and `database.create`, "Create databases" (PRM-215). The blueprint proposed `space.databases`; the name follows what it guards, making a database, because editing one stays `page.edit`.
- **One place that knows the kinds:** `permissions.CreatePermission(kind)`. Posts and databases ask for their own; pages, folders and links for `page.create`. `Evaluate` needed no new layer: the two behave like `page.create` (not in an archived space, not on an archived page, not stopped by an edit restriction), and the matrix holds that.
- **Every way to make an item** asks for the kind's permission: creating, at the top and below a parent; turning an unpublished page into a post (and back, which asks for `page.create`); copying, for every item of a copied branch; moving within a space; moving a branch to another space, for every kind in it. Templates, placeholders and imports only make pages and keep `page.create`.
- **Migration 0041:** `rbac_split_off` and `rbac_words` learn the two, and `rbac_split_roles()` gives them to every role and own set that holds `page.create`. The built-in Editor, Manager and Space admin hold them.
- **Bundles** are format 4. Formats 1 to 3 bring both with `page.create`.
- **The older grants route** completes a list with `page.create` and neither of the two (`WithSplitOff`).
- **The audit:** `page.created` names the kind.
- **Screens:** the Create button and its menu offer only what a person may make, and the button makes a post at once for someone who may only write posts. Copy, and turning into a post or back, show only with the right permission. The role editor lists both under pages with one line each, and "Create pages" now says what it covers.

## The migration proof

- `TestAdoptingGrantsChangesNoDecision`: grants of every shape from before roles, adopted into roles. 1,016,664 decisions compared, 68,830 allowed, 4,224 of those through a split; none changed. Before this wave: 949,740, 3,506 through a split.
- `TestTheMigrationGivesWhoeverCreatesPagesPostsAndDatabases`: the migration step on roles that exist before it. The built-in roles without the two, a role made by hand with page creation, an own set, and a person who never created pages. Afterwards everyone who created pages holds both, the other person holds exactly what they held, the built-in roles match their templates, and `rbac_drift()` is empty. An old list on the grants route gets both.
- `TestTheSplitIsTheSameInGoAndSQL` and `TestBuiltInRolesMatchTheTemplates` hold Go and SQL to one list.
- The matrix: 11,929,005 cases in `Evaluate` (was 10,046,790), three new grant cases (posts only, databases only, pages without either) and two new actions; the SQL half compares 96,444 pages.

## Tests

- `internal/app/posts_and_databases_test.go`: refusals first. Pages without the two cannot make a post or a database, at the top or below a page, and the space says so. Each kind is made with its own permission alone. Turning into a post asks for posts, back asks for pages, a folder for neither. Copying a database, or a branch with one, needs databases where the copy goes. Moving a branch with a database needs databases in the target. The created event names the kind.
- `internal/exchange/grants_test.go`: bundles of format 3 give page creators both; a current bundle keeps page creation alone.
- `web/src/create-permission.test.ts`: which permission makes which kind, and what is offered.
- `web/e2e/create-kinds.spec.ts`: a person who may only write posts is offered a post and nothing else, and Create opens the post dialog; a person without databases is not offered one; the role editor lists both.
- Mutations: twelve new ones, one per new check, and three older ones that named changed lines were updated. Two mutations of migration 0040 no longer bit, because 0041 redefines `rbac_split_off` and runs `rbac_split_roles()` again: "editors get no move out" now breaks the live definition in 0041, and "roles stay as they were" now breaks `rbac_split_roles` itself, which both migrations call. One new mutation did not compile at first and was rewritten.

## Load budget (ARC-238)

Making an item: unchanged, one check of a different permission. Turning into a post: one more check from facts in hand. A copy: one check per item from kept facts. A move to another space: one more query, the distinct kinds of the branch by primary key, and at most three checks. Capabilities: two more answers, no query. The migration rewrites the grants of everyone who holds page creation once, in one transaction, as migrations 0036 and 0040 did.

## Pictures

- `files` failed after midnight because it showed the real date a file was added. Main fixed that test in 2965bf7, which this branch carries; the picture is unchanged.

## Not proven

- A role held by thousands of subjects: the migration rewrites their grants in one transaction. Not measured at that size, as for 0036 and 0040.
- A large copy (over 50 pages) of a branch with a database, by someone without databases: the code reports that item as not copied and goes on; only the small copy, refused whole, is tested.
- The tree's drag to move offers itself with page creation, as before; dragging a database without `database.create` is refused by the server, not hidden.

## For the product owner

- The database permission is named "Create databases" and guards making one only. Editing a database stays with "Edit pages". If databases should also be edited apart, that is a new permission.
- Folders and links stay with "Create pages".
