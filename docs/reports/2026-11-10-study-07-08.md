# Study of blueprints 07 and 08

Written for: the session that builds the waves of spec 048.

## What was done

A study of blueprint 07 (operations and upgrades) and blueprint 08 (roadmap) at the depth of the studies of 03 to 06, before any more work on them. Documents only: nothing was run or built.

- `docs/blueprint/07-operations/`: nine chapters and sources. Lines OPS-070 to OPS-114 added to blueprint 07.
- `docs/blueprint/08-roadmap/`: four chapters and sources. Lines RMP-001 to RMP-033 added to blueprint 08.
- `docs/specs/048-study-of-07-and-08.md`: the findings, a review of wave O7, 15 decisions, 3 open questions, and 10 waves in build order.

## How the sources were chosen

Peer-reviewed papers where they exist (SOSP, OSDI, NSDI, ICSE, ESEC/FSE, IMC, USENIX Security, IEEE S&P, CCS), standards and official documentation next (PostgreSQL, NIST, ISO, FDA, W3C, IETF, EU regulations), and public post-mortems and upgrade guides of other projects for practice. The papers were checked against their publisher, index or author pages on 2026-11-10, and a fact that only a secondary page gave was left out or worded with care. Links to official documentation point to their usual places; check each again when a wave relies on it, since such pages move. For the enterprise document features, standards were read instead of any product, to keep the clean room.

## The findings that matter most for building

1. The upgrade test is the right kind of test, but checks reads and logins only, while research puts most upgrade failures in stored data and messages (wave 2).
2. The product does not yet show an operator the quiet failures: an old backup, a filling disk, a release far behind (wave 1).
3. Contract migrations run 10 minutes after every node knows them; the study decided 24 hours (D1).
4. Releases can be signed, packaged, described and checked for reproducibility with free tools and no long-lived key (wave 5).
5. Most of what blueprint 07 says "needs servers" can be done on virtual machines of one host (waves 7 and 8); section 8.4 of chapter 07.8 says what that cannot prove.
6. Document control comes first of the enterprise features, its requirements taken from ISO 9001, 21 CFR Part 11 and Annex 11.

## Not proven

Nothing here is measured; the waves measure. The numbers quoted from other projects (support windows, upgrade stops) are as their documentation stated on 2026-11-10 and change over time.

## Open

The three questions at the end of spec 048 are for the product owner: a payout route for donations that keeps the author anonymous, the S3 client library, and whether a small cluster on virtual machines is enough for the Helm chart.
