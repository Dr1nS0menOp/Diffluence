# Wave AR: roles on the Access screen, and typed text that outlives a reload (spec 043)

## What was built

- `permissions.PreviewSpaceRoles` and `POST /api/v1/spaces/{id}/role-assignments/preview`: what a choice of roles would give and take for one subject, from the exact union of the roles, with the same access check, subject check and limits as `SetSpaceRoles`. `PreviewGrant` and the new preview share `grantsOf` and `previewAgainst`.
- `web/src/views/access-roles.tsx`: the panel of people and groups with one role each, used on the Access screen and in the settings part People and roles. The matrix dialog, the template select and the checkbox dialog for space roles are gone; the instance-role list in the administration stays as it was.
- Combinations without a name: shown under their own name, with a line saying so; "Name this role" for administrators renames the role through `PUT /admin/roles/{id}` with the same permissions.
- `.role-count` keeps "0 holders" on one line.
- `before-reload.ts` gained `onBeforeReload`, `keepOpenAcrossReload` and `useOpenAfterReload`. Comment boxes keep their draft and reopen; `kept-fields.ts` keeps typed text of settings forms in `sessionStorage` and types it back after the reload, one field at a time.

## What the tests showed

- The Go test of the preview failed first (the method did not exist), then passed. A later check, that a manager cannot preview making an administrator, also failed first: the first version of the preview did not apply the limits, and would have shown a gain the save then refused. The preview now applies them.
- The browser tests found four things on the first run: the person names in the new rows were links too close together for WCAG 2.5.8 (target size), so names are now plain text there, as the People screen links them; a locator matched the page's own `article` as well as the comments; status locators matched the other live regions; and naming a combination keeps `page.move_out`, which the older grants route adds with `page.edit`, so the test now compares before and after instead of a fixed list.
- The first pictures showed the long name of a combination stretching its list and squeezing the person's name into one word per line. `.member-row .role-choice` now has a maximum width and the buttons do not wrap. Every changed picture was looked at: `space-access` (a reader: the note asks an administrator to name the combination), `space-roles` (an administrator: Name this role), `name-role` (the dialog), `admin-roles` ("0 holders" on one line) and `role-editor` (changed only behind the dialog).
- The frontend code was written before its browser tests, not after a failing one; the unit test of `parseKept` covers its failure paths first.

## Gates

- Rebased onto wave S6 before the gates, as asked; `scripts/mutation-check.sh` and `en.json` merged without a conflict and keep both sides. The CHANGELOG entry sits under the one of S6.
- `make check`: exit 0.
- `scripts/e2e`: 442 passed, 7 skipped, exit 0.
- Mutation check, changed mode (`MUTATION_JOBS=2`): all caught, including the four new mutations: anyone previews the roles of a space, a role preview ignores the roles chosen, a role preview promises what the change refuses, a preview hides that someone loses the space.
- Full mutation check (`MUTATION_JOBS=2`): 458 caught, no MISSED or BROKEN line; the log ends in "all mutations were caught".
- WSL restarted by itself during the first full mutation run, which took that run and the logs in `/tmp` with it. The containers were started again and the mutation check ran again on the same tree; `make check` and the e2e had finished before the restart and the tree did not change after them.

## Load budget (ARC-238)

In spec 043: two reads to open the screen, one short transaction per preview, saving unchanged, naming one update without any grant rewrite. The reload step costs no request. The list of assignments is not paged and was not measured past a few dozen rows.

## Not proven

- A space with hundreds of subjects holding roles: the list is unpaged.
- A settings screen whose data arrives after the 15 seconds of the restore window, or that resets its form from a later refetch after the text came back.
- Settings forms inside a dialog: the dialog is closed after the reload, so its text is not restored.
- A comment on a selection: its draft stays, but its box does not reopen.
