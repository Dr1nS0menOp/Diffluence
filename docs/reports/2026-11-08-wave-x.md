# Report: wave X, 8 November 2026

## Scope

The look and the settings of ADR-034, put first because a product that people do not like the look of is not taken up. Spec 033 holds every ask, the answer and the decisions D1 to D9. Permissions by role (RBAC) are not in this wave; the screens for access keep working as they were.

## What changed on screen

- **The Stil look.** White and blue with fewer lines: the sidebar, the top bar and the foot of the sidebar lose their borders, tiles become a soft surface without an outline, and tables keep only a line under each row and a quiet heading.
- **Sidebar on the personal screens.** Home, Recent, Favourites, Tasks, Watching and All spaces have a sidebar with a part "For you" and a part "Spaces".
- **Parts that fold and resize.** The parts of the sidebar fold with their heading; the part "This space" can be made higher or lower with a handle (mouse or the arrow keys, a double click resets). Both are kept per person on the server.
- **The tree.** No guide lines and no separate "Pages" heading: the tree follows the space part directly, folded from the first level. Its tools are a quiet row of icons above it, and the filter and the order unfold from the filter icon.
- **Labels** are plain accent text after a muted `#`, without an outline; the field to add one stays quiet until it is used.
- **Space settings** live in the left sidebar in groups that fold, with "Jump to a setting"; the large area on the right changes the chosen section, and every section has its own address.
- **The administration** is grouped as Overview; People and access; Content; Automation; Look; System, with Care and System health under System.
- **People of a space**: pictures, the role as a choice, how each person got access, tabs Everyone, Administrators, Groups and Guests, and a search. The user list in the administration filters by role, status and space.
- **Automation of the instance** acts in every space or in chosen spaces only, also for lookups that find pages.
- **The search box** always searches every space. Clicking it unfolds a panel with filters for spaces, type, labels, people and date; these become words of the existing syntax (`space:`, `label:`, `author:`) or the existing parameters of the results page, which shows them and keeps them. The space chooser shows the first spaces in the person's order, and a field finds any other space by name. The box is softer and rounded, and says "Search in" the name of the organisation when one is set, otherwise "Search".
- **The name of the organisation** can be given at the first setup (`diffluence user create-admin --organisation`) and changed under Administration, Look.

## Pictures

All 85 existing pictures of `0-design.spec.ts` were rewritten, because the borders, the sidebar on the personal screens, the search box and the folded tree reach every screen. One is new: `quick-search-filters`.

Checked by eye, after the fixes for overlapping parts and centred group headings, and again after the tree lost its heading:

- `space-people`, `page-light`, `page-dark`, `narrow`, `right-to-left`: the parts of the sidebar keep their own height, and the tree tools sit under Archive without touching it.
- `blocks-dark` and `editor-light` (a tree opened to a page, long sidebar), `overview` and `tree-collapsed` (a short tree): no part runs over another; the sidebar scrolls as a whole.
- `admin-summary`: group headings align at the start with their items, chevron first.
- `space-settings`, `rules`, `labels`, `space-access-people`, `quick-search`, `quick-search-filters`, `start`: the new layouts render as meant, and `start` shows "Search" without an organisation.

## Decisions taken in the wave

See D1 to D9 in spec 033. In short: the spaces of a rule live in its definition, so no migration was needed; the sidebar parts are server-side preferences; settings sections have addresses; search filters are words of the existing syntax so the coming query language can grow from them.

## Tests that changed with the screens

- Tests that read a child in the tree now open its branch first, because the tree starts folded. The current page's ancestors still open by themselves.
- Tests that looked for the tree or the shortcuts on a settings screen now look on the space, because settings have their own sidebar.
- Names that now appear twice are scoped: the top bar's Spaces menu against the sidebar part Spaces, and the button Archive against the settings group "Archive or delete".
- The test of the search filters found a gap: only the first 20 spaces could be chosen. The field that finds any space by name came from that, with a unit test written first.

## Not proven or left

- There is no first-run screen in the web app, so the organisation name is set through the command line or the Look settings only. The command-line flag has no automated test; the setting itself does.
- Type and date are filters but not yet words of the syntax; that belongs to the query language.
- The results page still has its own space select beside the `space:` words. Both work; joining them is left for the query language.
- The folded state of the groups in the settings sidebar lives in the screen only and is not kept.
- Right-to-left and contrast were checked on pictures, not with a screen reader.
- The test of the top bar's Spaces menu depends on the recent spaces of the shared test administrator, which parallel tests also change. It now visits its space just before it opens the menu, which narrows the window but does not close it.
