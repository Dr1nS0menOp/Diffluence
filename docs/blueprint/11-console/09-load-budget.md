# 11.9: Load budget

How to read this chapter: see [01-console-runs-the-installation.md](01-console-runs-the-installation.md).

Every feature states what it costs (ARC-238), and the installation keeps room for four times its database work (ADR-036). Running the installation from the console is rare work for a few people, so its budget is about two things: the steady cost of the worker and of the pages while open, which must be near nothing, and the heavy tasks (the restore test, a reseal, a prune), which must stay out of the way of people.

The figures used: the worker's round of spec 070 costs two indexed reads of `schema_migrations` and `nodes`, one read of the newest checkpoint, and the restore query; `backup next` costs one upsert, one settings read and two indexed lookups (spec 058). The real-data dump of wave PF has 5,001 people and 60,385 pages.

- **CSL-700** · new · **The worker's round** gains one upsert of `ops.workers` and one read of the oldest open task by the partial index (CSL-405): two statements a minute. With no task open the read finds nothing in one index probe. *Budget:* under 1 ms of database time a minute. *Proof:* a query-count test of one idle round.
- **CSL-701** · new · **The nodes' heartbeat** gains two small fields (key ids, log level), in the update it already makes every 15 seconds. *Budget:* no new statement. *Proof:* the query-count test of the heartbeat.
- **CSL-702** · new · **The log level and the thresholds** are read with maintenance mode, every five seconds per node, from the settings cache: one more key in a read that already runs. *Budget:* no new statement on a request path. *Proof:* the query budget test of `internal/app` keeps its numbers.
- **CSL-703** · new · **Pages while open** poll every 15 seconds, as the backup page does: the upgrade page reads `nodes`, `ops.workers` and `schema_migrations` (three small tables); the worker's page reads `ops.tasks` by `requested_at` with a limit of 50; the audit area reads `audit_signing_keys` and the newest `audit_witness_checks` row. *Budget:* each under 5 ms on the dump, in the reading class of ARC-233. *Proof:* benchmarks on the dump.
- **CSL-704** · new · **The prune preview** counts rows older than the cut-off through an index on the audit log's time (the wave checks that one exists, and adds it in its migration if not), and is cached for an hour. *Budget:* under 200 ms on an audit log of 10 million rows. *Proof:* a benchmark on a generated audit log of that size.
- **CSL-705** · new · **The Configuration page** reads the environment and the file, as today, and the other processes' lists from their rows. *Budget:* no database work beyond one read of `nodes` and `ops.workers`.
- **CSL-706** · new · **The restore test** reads the whole backup and writes a second copy of the database. It runs with one `pg_restore` job, at the lowest CPU and I/O priority the worker may set, at a quiet hour, at most once a day (CSL-512, CSL-515). *Budget:* on the real-data dump, done within **15 minutes**, and the p95 of reading pages during it within **25%** of its p95 without it. *Proof:* measured in the wave that builds it, on the dump, with the reading load of `scripts/load/reading.js`; the result goes into `limits.md`.
- **CSL-707** · new · **A reseal** works in runs of two minutes (as `key reseal` does) and sleeps between runs so that it takes at most **5% of the database's time** over a run; the page shows the expected end from the measured rate. *Budget:* on the dump with its files, the reseal of the values within an hour. *Proof:* measured in its wave.
- **CSL-708** · new · **A prune** deletes in batches of 10,000 rows, each its own transaction, with a short pause between, once a day. *Budget:* under 1% of the database's time over the run; no lock on `audit_events` longer than one batch. *Proof:* a benchmark on the generated audit log.
- **CSL-709** · new · **The events check** of the worker runs hourly and reads the event log's positions with its index (spec 068, L1 to L3). *Budget:* under 100 ms at a week's event log at four times the model (about 4 million rows). *Proof:* a benchmark.
- **CSL-710** · new · **Key loading without a restart** reads the key files once per change of the key generation, not per request. *Budget:* nothing on a request path. *Proof:* a test that a request does not read the key folder.
- **CSL-711** · new · **Mail** for tier-2 and tier-3 actions is one queued mail per system administrator per action, rare by nature. *Budget:* negligible.
- **CSL-712** · new · **The sum against ADR-036**: the steady cost of this whole study is a few statements a minute and two fields in an existing heartbeat, against the hundreds of statements a second of the model (spec 068, 3.2). The heavy tasks are bounded in time of day, rate and priority, and measured. Nothing here changes saving, reading or search.
