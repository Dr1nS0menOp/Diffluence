# Sources of blueprint 09

Standards and regulations, read for what they require:

- ISO: [ISO 9001:2015, Quality management systems, Requirements](https://www.iso.org/standard/62085.html), clause 7.5 "Documented information" (7.5.2 creating and updating, 7.5.3 control).
- US FDA: [21 CFR Part 11, Electronic Records; Electronic Signatures](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/chapter-I/subchapter-A/part-11): §11.10 controls for closed systems, §11.50 signature manifestations, §11.70 signature and record linking, §11.100 general requirements, §11.200 electronic signature components and controls.
- US FDA: [Guidance for Industry, Part 11, Electronic Records; Electronic Signatures, Scope and Application](https://www.fda.gov/regulatory-information/search-fda-guidance-documents/part-11-electronic-records-electronic-signatures-scope-and-application), 2003.
- European Commission: [EudraLex Volume 4, Annex 11, Computerised Systems](https://health.ec.europa.eu/system/files/2016-11/annex11_01-2011_en_0.pdf), 2011: sections 9 (audit trails), 11 (periodic evaluation) and 14 (electronic signature).
- European Union: [Regulation (EU) No 910/2014 (eIDAS)](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2014/910/oj), articles 3, 25 and 26 on electronic, advanced and qualified electronic signatures.
- W3C: [Web Content Accessibility Guidelines 2.2](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/).
- W3C: [Web Authentication Level 3](https://www.w3.org/TR/webauthn-3/), user verification.
- OpenID Foundation: [OpenID Connect Core 1.0](https://openid.net/specs/openid-connect-core-1_0.html), `prompt=login` and `max_age`, for a fresh login at signing.

Behaviour: blueprint 08 names a document-management product only to say which behaviour is meant. This study did not read its pages; every requirement here comes from the standards above and from Diffluence's own code and blueprints.
