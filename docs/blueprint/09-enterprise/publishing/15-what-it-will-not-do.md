# 09.P15: What this feature will not do

How to read this chapter: see [01-draft-and-published-spaces.md](01-draft-and-published-spaces.md).

A study that only lists what a feature does invites every later wish to slip in. This chapter lists what draft and published spaces will **not** do in spec 073, each with its reason, and whether it is **not** (decided against) or **later** (possible, waiting for need). RMP-005 asks every phase to state this.

- **PUB-1500** · not · **Two draft spaces into one published space.** One source per published page, one answer to "where do I change this?" (PUB-1050).
- **PUB-1501** · not · **One draft space into several published spaces**, one per audience. It turns a pair into a fan, with pages chosen per audience and links that may resolve in one audience and not in another; it is the product owner's to decide (01, 1.4). Today, two pairs from two draft spaces, or one published space with restrictions per page, cover the need.
- **PUB-1502** · not · **Chains**, where a published space is the draft space of another pair (PUB-1052).
- **PUB-1503** · later · **Releasing into another installation.** Linked instances are phase 8 (blueprint 08), which starts as one-way, revocable copies (RMP-022); this pair is its inside-one-installation form, and its page pairs are a model for it.
- **PUB-1504** · not · **Editing in the published space**, and so **merging** edits made there back into the draft. The published space is managed (PUB-1403); a correction is made in the draft and released.
- **PUB-1505** · not · **Copying comments**, reactions, watchers, favourites, page status, page owner, restrictions or version history across the pair (chapter 04).
- **PUB-1506** · later · **Blog posts** in a pair (PUB-231).
- **PUB-1507** · not · **Databases** in a pair (PUB-232).
- **PUB-1508** · not · **Templates** in a published space (PUB-233).
- **PUB-1509** · not · **Releasing part of a page**, such as one section. A release is of whole pages; a section that must wait goes in a page of its own or waits in the draft.
- **PUB-1510** · later · **Scheduled releases** at a date and time. Spec 011's scheduled publishing shows the way (rights at the moment of the run); it waits until teams ask, since a release manager can release at the moment today.
- **PUB-1511** · later · **Undo of a run** as one act (PUB-456).
- **PUB-1512** · not · **Releasing the working version** of a controlled page (PUB-700).
- **PUB-1513** · not · **Workflows in the published space** (PUB-703).
- **PUB-1514** · later · **Read and understood on twins** and **review dates shown to readers** (PUB-733, PUB-734), after DC5.
- **PUB-1515** · not · **Fixing links silently** when their target is released later (PUB-1412).
- **PUB-1516** · later · **Folding search results** of a draft page under its twin (PUB-805).
- **PUB-1517** · not · **A public site of the draft space** (PUB-1417).
