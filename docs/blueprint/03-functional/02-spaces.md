# 03.2: Spaces

A space is the unit of ownership: it holds a tree of pages, a blog, files and its own permissions. Every page lives in exactly one space.

## 2.1 Creating a space

- **SPC-001** · built · A person with *create spaces* creates a space with a name and a key. Description and icon are optional.
- **SPC-002** · built · A key has 2 to 32 letters, digits, dashes or underscores, and is unique ignoring case.
- **SPC-003** · built · The key is suggested from the name while typing, and can be changed before saving. The form says at once when a key is taken.
- **SPC-004** · built · The creator becomes space administrator, and a home page is created.
- **SPC-005** · built · A space can start from a permission set: *Team space* (all members can edit) or *Read-only knowledge base* (all members can view).
- **SPC-006** · built · A space can start from a space template, which brings a home page, a set of pages, templates and labels (ORG-055).
- **SPC-007** · built · When a space is created, the creating form shows who will have access, so nobody creates an open space by accident.
- **SPC-008** · built · An administrator can set the default permission set of new spaces.
- **SPC-088** · new · Beyond SPC-008, the default access of new spaces is a list in the administration: people, groups and the classes all members and all guests, each with the role it gets in every new space, chosen in its row. The list applies to spaces made from then on; existing spaces do not change, and the creator still becomes space administrator (SPC-004). How this list and the starting sets of SPC-005 combine needs its own study.
- **SPC-089** · new · The screen of that list has a heading with one line of explanation and a help link, and a button to add receivers at the end of the heading's row. Below it a table with three columns: the receiver, the role, and an unnamed column for each row's **More** menu, which offers Remove. Rows stay in the order they were added, and the explanation of roles in the administration links to this screen.
- **SPC-090** · new · On that screen a receiver shows a picture or group mark, the name and a second line, which for a group is the line of ACC-126. The role is picked in the row from an unboxed choice with an arrow, and a combination without a name shows muted under its short name (PRM-218), with a button beside it that explains what it allows.

## 2.2 Details of a space

- **SPC-009** · built · A space administrator edits the name, description and icon.
- **SPC-010** · built · An icon is an animal or one character. Without a choice, the key of the space picks an animal.
- **SPC-011** · built · An icon can be an uploaded image, cropped to a square.
- **SPC-012** · built · The key of a space can be changed by a space administrator. Old addresses that contain the key keep working through a redirect.
- **SPC-013** · built · A space has a list of owners shown to everyone, so people know who to ask.
- **SPC-014** · built · Categories can be set on a space (NAV-052).
- **SPC-015** · built · A space administrator picks which page is the home page.
- **SPC-016** · built · The home page cannot be deleted.
- **SPC-091** · new · The **Details** section of space settings is one form, in this order: the space's badge, large; a line that says how required fields are marked; the name, the key and the owners, all three required; the administrators and who created the space, both read only; a fold for more details, closed by default; and Save with Reset changes at the end (SPC-152). It holds what the earlier sections Details, Space key, Space owners and Space categories held, and the choice of home page.
- **SPC-092** · new · The badge at the top of Details shows the space's animal, character or image without a control to change it; it is changed in the section Picture of the space (SPC-087, SPC-010, SPC-011).
- **SPC-093** · new · Right after the key field, an information button explains what a change of key does: old addresses keep working through a redirect (SPC-012), and the old key stays taken until a space administrator frees it, which is done in the same form. For a personal space the key comes from the person (SPC-077), and the explanation says whether it can change.
- **SPC-094** · new · The owners field in Details is a people field that takes one or more people (SPC-013), each with a small picture before the name; at least one owner is required.
- **SPC-095** · new · Under the owners, read only: the space's administrators as up to five overlapping pictures and a quiet link to all of them, with no count and no add button, which opens People filtered to the administrator role; and who created the space, as their name linking to their profile, with the date under it in small muted text (day, short month, year). Administrators are changed in People, not here.
- **SPC-096** · new · The fold for more details is the fold control of NAV-169, under the read-only part. Opened, it shows the description, the categories (SPC-014) and the home page (SPC-015) as fields of the same form.

## 2.3 The overview of a space

- **SPC-017** · built · Opening a space shows its home page.
- **SPC-018** · built · The overview shows the space name, description, owners and the number of pages.
- **SPC-019** · built · It shows recent changes in the space and the most viewed pages, each of which can be turned off. Still open: most viewed pages (wave H), and turning parts off.
- **SPC-020** · built · It shows the space's newest blog posts when the space has a blog.
- **SPC-021** · built · A person can favourite and watch the space from here.

## 2.4 The sidebar of a space

- **SPC-022** · built · The sidebar shows the space badge and name, a button to hide it, and the page tree.
- **SPC-023** · built · The sidebar can be hidden and comes back at the same state after a reload.
- **SPC-024** · built · The sidebar can be made wider or narrower by dragging its edge. The width is remembered per person.
- **SPC-025** · partly · A section **Shortcuts** holds links chosen by space administrators: to pages, other spaces or outside addresses, each with its own label and an order that can be changed by dragging. Still open: dragging: Move up and Move down do the job.
- **SPC-026** · partly · Fixed entries sit above the tree: **Overview**, **Blog** when turned on, **Calendar** later, and **Files** (FIL-043). Still open: Calendar.
- **SPC-027** · built · Space administrators see links to Space settings and Trash at the bottom.
- **SPC-028** · built · A **More** button by the space name opens: Watch this space, Copy link, Export, Space settings.
- **SPC-029** · built · The sidebar has a filter on page titles. Typing narrows the tree to matching rows with their parents, and matching words are marked.
- **SPC-030** · built · A view switch offers **Tree**, **Last visited by me**, **Last updated** and **A to Z**. The chosen view stays. Only the tree can be rearranged, and the other views say so.
- **SPC-031** · built · A long tree loads branches as they open, so a space with 50,000 pages still opens at once.
- **SPC-082** · built · A fixed **Home** row sits above Overview and the tree. It is marked while the home page is open.
- **SPC-083** · built · The home page is not a row of the tree, the flat views or the filter. Its children are the top rows, followed by pages without a parent.
- **SPC-084** · built · On the home page the breadcrumbs end in **Home**.
- **SPC-085** · built · A strip of spaces at the top of the sidebar moves a person between spaces: favourites, then recent spaces, then the rest, as in the Spaces menu. It scrolls on its own, by wheel and by keyboard, and has a link to all spaces.
- **SPC-086** · built · The strip marks the current space and keeps it in view, also when the space is archived.
- **SPC-087** · built · A space administrator picks one of 30 cartoon animals in Space settings, and a person picks one when making a space.
- **SPC-097** · new · Inside a space, the block of the space follows the global part of the sidebar (chapter 1) after a line, in this order: the space's header row, **Shortcuts**, **Content** with its filter and the tree, **Blogs**, **Calendars** and **Space apps** (changes the design standard, decided 2026-11-14).
- **SPC-098** · new · The header row of the space holds its badge, its name and a **More** button at the end that is always shown, not only on hover. The row leads to the home page and takes the place of the Home row of SPC-082: while the home page is open it is marked as the current row, with the accent tint and rounded corners, and while another page is open it is plain (changes the design standard, decided 2026-11-14).
- **SPC-099** · new · Each group of the space's block has a heading of an icon and a word, as large as a row and in regular weight, at the block's start edge, with its buttons at the end of the heading in one column shared by all headings. The rows of a group are indented one step under the heading, and the markers of the tree, the plus of the Create row and the icons of shortcuts and app entries share one column. An empty group shows one muted sentence that starts where the heading's word starts (changes the design standard, decided 2026-11-14).
- **SPC-100** · new · Beyond SPC-025, the **Shortcuts** heading has an add button at its end, shown only to those who may change shortcuts, which opens the form of the Shortcuts section of space settings in a dialog, so a shortcut is added without leaving the space. A shortcut row is indented under the heading and shows the icon of what it points to in the markers' column, then its label; a shortcut may point to a page that also stands in the tree.
- **SPC-101** · new · The **Content** heading carries two buttons at its end: add, which opens the create menu (SPC-112), and order and view, which opens a menu with the views of SPC-030 and the tools collapse all and show the current page (SPC-042) and select (SPC-043). The title filter of SPC-029 is a field that is always shown, directly under the heading and across the block, and it is the only boxed element of the block; the row of tool icons above the tree goes (changes the design standard, decided 2026-11-14).
- **SPC-102** · new · Once the sidebar is scrolled down, a small half-transparent control with an arrow and a word floats over the top of its content; it brings the sidebar back to its start and puts focus on the space's header row. It is absent while the sidebar is at its top, works from the keyboard, and scrolls without motion when reduced motion is asked for (NAV-081).
- **SPC-103** · new · Beyond SPC-026, when the blog is on, a **Blogs** group follows the Create row of the tree. Its heading leads to the list of posts (ORG-065) and has an add button for those who may write a post (`post.create`, ADR-038); the newest posts follow as rows, newest first, and with none the group shows one muted sentence. How many posts show, and whether they group by year and month, needs its own study.
- **SPC-104** · later · Once calendars exist (OTH-015) and the calendar is on, a **Calendars** group follows Blogs: a heading that leads to the space's calendars, with no button of its own.
- **SPC-105** · later · Once apps exist (PLT-039), a **Space apps** group comes last in the block: its heading carries the apps icon and a **More** button at its end, and one indented entry per app the space uses, each with the app's own small icon and its name, opens that app's screen for the space.
- **SPC-106** · new · The fixed entries of SPC-026, the links at the bottom of SPC-027, the Home row of SPC-082 and the strip of spaces of SPC-085 and SPC-086 leave the sidebar. The home page is reached by the header row, the blog by the Blogs group, the calendar by the Calendars group; Overview, People, Files, Archived pages and Space settings by the space menu (SPC-107); the trash through space settings; and another space through the Spaces entry of the global part of the sidebar (chapter 1) (changes the design standard, decided 2026-11-14).
- **SPC-107** · new · Beyond SPC-028, the space menu holds four blocks split by lines: Favourite space and Watch space, whose words follow the current state, then Copy link; under a small heading for the space's tools, Overview, People, Files and Archived pages; Export, Automation and Space settings; and Archive space, or Restore space when it is archived, then Delete space in the danger style. Each entry shows only to those who may use it (NAV-004), and Automation only while that experimental part is on (ADR-043).
- **SPC-108** · new · The space menu opens downward from the header row's **More** button, over the sidebar's edge, and the button keeps an accent outline while it is open. Every entry has an icon before its name and no sub-menu arrow, key hint or badge, and a favourite space shows a filled star. People opens the People section for those who manage access and the list of SPC-058 for everyone else; Archive and Delete ask as SPC-068 and SPC-073 say.
- **SPC-160** · new · Beyond SPC-105, the **Space apps** group shows already before apps exist, whenever one of Diffluence's own optional parts is on in the space: it lists that part's screen for the space, such as the drawings of the space (BLK-064), release to a published space (PUB-1200) and document control (09-enterprise), each as an indented entry with its icon and name, before any app. A part that is off has no entry (ADR-043), an entry shows only to those who may use it (NAV-004), and the **More** button on the heading lets a space administrator choose which entries show. What else that menu offers, and what the screen of the drawings of a space lists, needs its own study.

## 2.5 The page tree

- **SPC-032** · built · Pages form a tree of any depth with a manual order.
- **SPC-033** · built · A branch has a chevron and can be collapsed. Guide lines show the structure.
- **SPC-034** · built · The first two levels start open. What a reader collapses stays collapsed, per space, across pages and reloads.
- **SPC-035** · built · Going to a page reveals it, also inside a collapsed branch.
- **SPC-036** · built · The current page has a tint and shows the add button. Other rows show it on hover or focus.
- **SPC-037** · built · A person who may move pages can drag a row onto another page to make it a child, or between rows to reorder it.
- **SPC-038** · built · A **More** button on each row offers: Open in new tab, Copy link, Rename, Move, Copy, Favourite, Watch, Archive, Delete. Each is shown only to those who may.
- **SPC-039** · built · Hovering over or focusing a row shows a small card with the owner, the last change and the status. It can be turned off in the preferences.
- **SPC-040** · built · The row shows the page's emoji (PAG-123), a lock when it has a restriction, and a draft mark when the person has an unpublished change.
- **SPC-041** · built · The current person's unpublished pages appear in the tree in a muted style, only for them.
- **SPC-042** · built · A button collapses all branches at once, and another reveals the current page.
- **SPC-043** · built · Several rows can be selected with Shift or Ctrl and moved together.
- **SPC-044** · built · A row can be dragged to another space in the Spaces menu, with the same rules as a move (PAG-042).
- **SPC-045** · built · A long drag scrolls the tree and opens a collapsed branch after a short wait.
- **SPC-046** · built · A page cannot be moved below itself, and two people moving pages at once cannot create a loop.
- **SPC-109** · new · Every row of the tree holds, in this order: a marker in a lead column, a chevron for a branch (pointing to the end side when shut and down when open) or a small dot for an item without children; the icon of the item's kind (page, folder, database, link entry), or the page's emoji in its place (PAG-123); the title; and on the same line after it the marks of SPC-040, such as the draft lozenge. The markers line up in one column, and all rows have one even height (changes the design standard, decided 2026-11-14).
- **SPC-110** · new · Beyond SPC-036, the open page's row is marked as NAV-144 says. No row shows its buttons at rest, the open row included: every row shows them on hover or focus, in place of the add button that SPC-036 shows on the current row (changes the design standard, decided 2026-11-14).
- **SPC-111** · new · Under the last row, a **Create** row closes the tree for those who may create something in the space: a plus in the markers' column, then the word. Pressed, or while its menu is open, it shows as a rounded box across the block with an accent outline.
- **SPC-112** · new · The Create row and the add button of the Content heading open the create menu, a card under the row, aligned to its start and about half the sidebar wide: Live document (once it exists, OTH-001), Page, Whiteboard (once it exists, OTH-006), Database (while that experimental part is on, ADR-043) and Link entry (ORG-072), then a line, then Folder. Each item is one line of an icon and a name, with no description, key hint or sub-menu, and the item under the pointer is tinted over its full width; the new item goes at the top level of the tree. A blog post starts from the Blogs group (SPC-103), so the kinds of OTH-024 stay and only the place where they are offered changes (changes the design standard, decided 2026-11-14).
- **SPC-113** · new · A section **Order of pages** in space settings shows the whole tree of the space, folded from the first level, under its heading and one line that says any item can be dragged to a new place. Its rows show the marker, kind icon and title of SPC-109; there is no save button, no drag handle at rest and no sort button. The home page is not a row, its children are the top rows (SPC-083), and only rows the person may see appear (NAV-006).
- **SPC-114** · new · In Order of pages, a person who may move pages drags a row between two rows or onto another item, or uses Move up, Move down, Indent and Outdent from the keyboard. Each move applies at once, with the same rules, checks and summaries as a move in the sidebar (SPC-037, SPC-046, PAG-043, PAG-044). Whether a very large space needs paging here (SPC-031) needs its own study.

## 2.6 Features of a space

- **SPC-047** · built · A space administrator turns features on or off per space: Blog, Comments, Reactions, Page statuses, Analytics, Automation, Calendar and later content types.
- **SPC-048** · built · Turning a feature off hides it. It never deletes content. Turning it on brings everything back.
- **SPC-049** · built · An administrator can set which features are on by default, and can turn a feature off for the whole instance.
- **SPC-115** · new · Beyond SPC-047, the **Features** section shows its switches in one card, in three groups each under a heading band: features of the space (Blog, Calendar, Comments, Reactions, Page statuses, Analytics, Automation), kinds of item (Database, Folder and Link entry, and Live document and Whiteboard once they exist) and parts of the sidebar (Shortcuts, and Apps once apps exist). Each row has the name at the start and its switch at the end; names in the first two groups have an icon before them, those in the third none. Pages are always on and are not in the list.
- **SPC-116** · new · A switch in Features applies at once, with no save button, hint, help link, badge or lock. A switch for an experimental part shows only while the installation has that part on (ADR-043, SPC-049), and one the instance turned off is absent (NAV-004).
- **SPC-117** · new · Turning a kind of item off takes it out of the create menu of the space (SPC-112), and turning a part of the sidebar off hides that group; nothing is deleted (SPC-048). What happens to existing items of a kind that is off, in the tree, in links and in search, needs its own study.

## 2.7 Permissions of a space

- **SPC-050** · built · A space has a matrix of permissions per person or group, and for "all members" and "all guests".
- **SPC-051** · built · The permissions are: view, export, administer; create, edit, archive, delete own and delete any page; add, delete own and delete any comment; add, delete own and delete any attachment; manage restrictions.
- **SPC-052** · built · Viewer, Editor and Space administrator are named sets that fill in the boxes.
- **SPC-053** · built · A space always keeps at least one direct person or group with *administer*.
- **SPC-054** · built · A fourth set, **Manager**, can manage people and content but not the settings of the space.
- **SPC-055** · built · Administrators can define their own named sets for the whole instance.
- **SPC-056** · built · The screen can be searched by name, and shows how each person gets their access: directly, or through which group.
- **SPC-057** · built · A change shows a summary of who gains and who loses what before it is saved.
- **SPC-058** · built · A person can see who has access to a space, without seeing the matrix, when they are allowed to view it.
- **SPC-059** · built · A space administrator can export the list of who has access as CSV.
- **SPC-118** · new · The **People** section of space settings lists everyone who holds a role in the space, groups, people and service accounts, one row each: a checkbox, a picture or group mark, the name with a second line, the role chosen in the row with a button beside it that explains what the role allows and how the holder got it, and a **More** button at the end. Only the holder and role columns have a heading, and a checkbox in the header row selects all rows (changes the design standard, decided 2026-11-14).
- **SPC-119** · new · In People, the second line of a group is the line of ACC-126; of a person, their e-mail address, only when they show it to others (ACC-023); of a service account, that it is one (ACC-043). A guest keeps the Guest mark beside the name.
- **SPC-120** · new · Above the list of People, in one row: a search by name at the start, then two dropdowns that filter by kind of holder and by role, nothing chosen at first, and at the end a button to add people, groups and service accounts. The dropdowns take the place of the tabs Everyone, Administrators, Groups and Guests of spec 033.
- **SPC-121** · new · In People, a holder with several roles shows them one at a time, as the Access screen does (ADR-040), and a combination without a name shows muted under its short name (PRM-218). Each row's **More** menu offers Remove access, and a **More** button at the end of the section's title line offers Export as CSV (SPC-059).
- **SPC-122** · new · In People, a person who may manage access selects rows with their checkboxes, and gives the selected holders one role or removes their access in one step, after the summary of SPC-057. Selected rows have the accent tint and a real checkbox, and a change that would leave the space without an administrator is refused (SPC-053). Which other actions a selection offers, and how many rows one change may hold, need their own study.
- **SPC-123** · new · After the upgrade that turns single permissions into roles, the People section shows a note under its description, with an icon and a bold title: what changed, that nobody gained or lost access, and how older combinations are named, with a help link. It is a note of news (NAV-150): a space administrator dismisses it, and it stays dismissed for them on every device (ACC-026).
- **SPC-124** · later · A role in a space can be given to all administrators of the instance as one class, which follows who holds administrator. It is an explicit grant that People and the Access screen show like any other and the audit log records, and the instance never adds it by itself, so administrators still do not read content by default (decision 2); like all members and all guests it never appears on a restriction (PRM-051). Whether system administrators count needs its own study.

## 2.8 Clearing up a space

Space administrators need a way to keep a growing space clean.

- **SPC-060** · partly · A **Content** screen in space settings lists all published pages in a table with title, owner, status, labels, created, last changed and last viewed. Still open: last viewed (wave H).
- **SPC-061** · built · It filters by owner (including disabled people), status, label, contributor, last changed, created, and "not active for 1, 3, 6 or 12 months". Pages with children are left out of the "not active" filter by default, so a parent is not removed by mistake. See spec 030.
- **SPC-062** · built · Rows are selected with checkboxes. Selecting a parent selects its children, and they can be deselected one by one.
- **SPC-063** · built · Actions on a selection: archive, delete, change owner, change status, add or remove a label, move.
- **SPC-064** · built · Each action shows what it will do, including how many pages, before it runs. Bulk actions run in the background and report what succeeded and what did not.
- **SPC-065** · built · A filter lists pages with a restriction, so space administrators can review them.
- **SPC-066** · built · A filter lists pages with links to pages that no longer exist.
- **SPC-067** · built · A filter lists pages with no incoming links and that are far from the top of the tree, as a hint that they may be lost.
- **SPC-125** · new · A section **Pages nothing leads to** lists the pages of SPC-067: no other page links to them and they sit far from the top of the tree. Its columns are title, creator, created, last changed by and last changed, then an actions column with Move, Archive and Delete for those who may. It shows only pages the person may see, and when empty it says what would appear here (NAV-007).
- **SPC-126** · new · A section **Pages still to write** lists each title that pages link to but that does not exist yet (EDT-064), one row per title, in two columns: the missing title, and the pages that link to it. A person who may create pages starts the missing page from its row with that title, under the linking page. Linking pages the person may not see are left out, and so is a title that only such pages link to.
- **SPC-127** · new · Pages still to write stays in the list of sections when there is nothing to show, and then says in one sentence what would appear (NAV-007), where spec 023 D8 showed the list only when it had rows.
- **SPC-128** · later · A space administrator downloads as CSV a list of the space's pages, or of its templates, that hold content an import kept as text with a note (EXC-036) or blocks this release does not know (EDT-158), to review and fix them. The section offers pages (the default) or templates as two tiles, a note that what the person may not see is left out, and one button that downloads; nothing is listed on screen, and a cell that would start a formula is made harmless.

## 2.9 Archiving and deleting a space

- **SPC-068** · built · A space administrator archives a space. It becomes read-only for everyone and can be restored.
- **SPC-069** · built · An archived space disappears from the Spaces menu, the directory and quick search. Direct addresses still work, with a banner that says the space is archived.
- **SPC-070** · built · Search can include archived spaces through a filter.
- **SPC-071** · built · A space administrator deletes a space. It goes to the trash for the universal retention period.
- **SPC-072** · built · Administrators restore or permanently remove spaces from the trash in the admin console.
- **SPC-073** · built · Deleting a space asks the person to type the key, and offers archiving and an export first.
- **SPC-074** · built · A key stays taken while its space is in the trash.
- **SPC-075** · new · The progress of a permanent removal is shown, because a large space takes time.
- **SPC-129** · new · The section **Archive the space** explains in an information note, with a help link, that the space becomes read-only for everyone, leaves the Spaces menu, the directory and quick search, can still be found through a search filter, and can be restored at any time (SPC-068 to SPC-070). One primary button named for the action archives it after a confirmation; there are no options and no field for a reason.
- **SPC-130** · new · For an archived space, the section Archive the space says that the space is archived, and its one primary button is Restore.
- **SPC-131** · new · The section **Delete the space** explains that the space goes to the trash for the retention period, from which an administrator can restore it (SPC-071, SPC-072), and offers archiving and an export first. Its one button is in the danger style and asks for the key (SPC-073).

## 2.10 Personal spaces

- **SPC-076** · built · A person with *create personal space* has one personal space, created on request from the account menu.
- **SPC-077** · built · Its key comes from the person and cannot clash with team spaces. It is listed under Personal in the directory.
- **SPC-078** · built · The owner is its administrator, and it starts private. They choose whether others can see it.
- **SPC-079** · partly · A personal space is a safe place to prepare a page and later move it to a team space. Still open: moving a page out, with move between spaces (wave D).
- **SPC-080** · built · When a person is disabled, their personal space stays. An administrator can hand it over or archive it.
- **SPC-081** · built · Space settings are the same as for team spaces, except that the owner can never be removed by a group change.
- **SPC-132** · new · A personal space shows its owner: its name is the owner's display name, and its badge is the owner's picture, or their initials on their tint when they have none, wherever the space shows. When the owner changes their name or picture, the space follows; the owner can still choose an animal or a character instead (SPC-087), and every other space keeps the animal of ADR-031. Whether the owner may rename the space, and what it shows once the owner is disabled or anonymised (SPC-080, ACC-028), needs its own study.
- **SPC-133** · new · The home page of a personal space shows the owner's picture, large, before its title; the other pages of the space have nothing there.

## 2.11 The settings of a space

Space settings are a frame of their own: a sidebar that lists every section in a fixed order, and a large area that shows one section at a time.

- **SPC-134** · new · Space settings open from the space menu (SPC-107) in the Settings frame: on the start side a sidebar of sections, about a quarter of the width and the full height, with a thin line between it and the main area, in the place of the space's sidebar; beside it the large area. A person sees only the sections they hold the permission for (spec 036), and someone who holds none sees no Space settings at all (changes the design standard, decided 2026-11-14).
- **SPC-135** · new · One section shows at a time in the large area, at its own address, so a link, a reload or Back lands on the same section (spec 033 D6). A section that is a screen of its own, such as Content, Trash or Automation, opens in the same large area with the settings sidebar beside it, where spec 033 made such sections links to other screens.
- **SPC-136** · new · The head of the settings sidebar holds, in this order: a back arrow with the title Space settings in bold, which returns to the space; a row with the space's badge and name, with no star and no menu; and a field across the sidebar that jumps to a setting. The head stays in place while the list scrolls under it, once its own top margin has scrolled away, and it is opaque with no line or shadow at its lower edge; the whole column has a scrollbar of its own and scrolls apart from the main area (changes the design standard, decided 2026-11-14).
- **SPC-137** · new · Typing in the jump field narrows the list to the sections whose names match, opening the groups that hold them, and Enter opens the first match; with no match the list says so in one muted sentence. Whether it also finds a setting inside a section, as the administration's search does (ADM-003), needs its own study.
- **SPC-138** · new · Below the head stand four sections on their own, each with an icon, in this order: **Automation**, the rules of the space (PLT-022); **People** (SPC-118); **Content**, the screen of SPC-060; and **Details** (SPC-091). A divider as wide as the jump field follows, with space before and after it, and then five groups that fold, in this order: General, Access, Pages and files, Look, Connections (changes the design standard, decided 2026-11-14).
- **SPC-139** · new · The group **General** holds, in this order: Features (SPC-115), Archive the space (SPC-129), Delete the space (SPC-131), Export (EXC-001 to EXC-011), Import (EXC-020), Import notes (SPC-128, later), Related content (later, chapter 3), Picture of the space (SPC-087, SPC-010, SPC-011), Statuses and approvals (SPC-140), Workflows (DOC-900) and Insights (PLT-016 to PLT-018).
- **SPC-140** · new · **Statuses and approvals** holds what the earlier sections Page statuses and Rules for pages held for pages: the space's statuses and whether editors may type their own (PAG-055, PAG-056), the review period that sets a review date when a page is first published (PAG-060), and whether publishing needs approval, with its fixed approvers (PAG-068). Workflows follows it as an entry of its own, beside the approval rule that a workflow replaces once one exists (DOC-900).
- **SPC-141** · new · The group **Access** holds, in this order: Permissions, the full Access screen (SPC-050); Public site (EXC-038); Single shared pages, where the space allows them (EXC-044, EXC-085); and Audit of the space (ADM-045). There is no section that lets visitors in without logging in and none for public links into the instance (decision 1, ADR-010): the public site and shared pages take their place.
- **SPC-142** · new · The group **Pages and files** holds, in this order: Order of pages (SPC-113), Pages nothing leads to (SPC-125), Pages still to write (SPC-126), Files (FIL-043), Trash (PAG-080), Restricted pages (SPC-065), Archived pages (PAG-073), Labels (ORG-040), Shortcuts (SPC-025), and Published space while that part is on (PUB-1200).
- **SPC-143** · new · The group **Look** holds, in this order: Look of the space (SPC-156, later), Templates (TPL-1000), Layout, PDF design (EXC-012) and Watermark (later, chapter 6). Layout holds the default title alignment for new pages, which Rules for pages held (PAG-126), and the header and footer of the space (SPC-158).
- **SPC-144** · new · The group **Connections** holds, in this order: Webhooks (PLT-038), a feed of the space (COL-081, later), linked instances once they reach a space (PLT-049, phase 8), and last the sections that apps add (PLT-039, phase 7), which look exactly like built-in sections. A section Diffluence builds itself sits in the group of its meaning, never here.
- **SPC-145** · new · The earlier groups Who can do what, Automation, Import and export, and Archive or delete give way to this grouping, and the section Keep it tidy goes, because every screen it linked to is now a section of its own. Every earlier section keeps a place: Details, Space key, Space owners, Space categories and the home page in Details; People and roles in People; Who has access in Access; Rules for pages in Statuses and approvals and in Layout; Archive or delete the space as two sections in General. This changes the groups of spec 033 and the place of Workflows in DOC-900.
- **SPC-146** · new · In the settings sidebar, the sections of a group are indented one step past its heading, as text without icons, in compact rows of one height. A group heading sits further to the start, with a thin chevron in a column of its own (down when open, toward the end side when shut), in the same weight and colour as the sections. All groups start open, several can be open at once whatever is selected, there are no lines between groups and nothing follows the last section, and the list keeps its scroll position from section to section (changes the design standard, decided 2026-11-14).
- **SPC-147** · new · Beyond NAV-144 and NAV-145, the band of the current section in the settings sidebar reaches to the end of the jump field and starts at the sidebar's start for a pinned section and at the chevrons' column for a grouped one; a section or group heading under the pointer gets a tint of the same shape (changes the design standard, decided 2026-11-14).
- **SPC-148** · new · The large area of a settings section is placed as SPC-150 says, with no breadcrumb and no save bar at the top. In order it holds: the section's name as the largest heading, with the section's **More** button or its primary action at the end of the same line; the description with its help link (NAV-113); notes; a row of tools with search and filters at the start and the add action at the end; and then the content (changes the design standard, decided 2026-11-14).
- **SPC-149** · new · A settings section with parts gives each part a visible sub-heading, its own explanatory line right under it and a larger gap above it, set apart by space alone, without cards or lines; heading, sub-headings and content share one start edge. This changes spec 033 D7, under which the first heading of a section only speaks to screen readers (changes the design standard, decided 2026-11-14).
- **SPC-150** · new · A settings section has one of two widths: the full width for tables, galleries and builders such as Automation, starting beside the sidebar, and a bounded column of reading width for forms and short lists such as Details, People, PDF design and Watermark, centred in the large area as in the administration (ADM-078). Nothing runs past the window: a wide table scrolls in its own box (changes the design standard, decided 2026-11-14).
- **SPC-151** · new · Beyond NAV-160, NAV-165 and NAV-168, a settings form of a space has one field per row with generous gaps; a choice among a few options is a set of bordered tiles, each with a bold title and a muted line, the chosen one with a filled radio; a number on a scale is the slider of ADM-100, with its number field at the end of its row; and a form with a preview shows it as ADM-139 says, beside the form on wide screens under a heading level with the first label, and under the form on narrow ones (changes the design standard, decided 2026-11-14).
- **SPC-152** · new · A settings form of a space that does not apply at once saves with Save and Reset changes, as NAV-162 and NAV-163 say.
- **SPC-153** · new · A section that starts an action, such as archiving, exporting or a report, holds in order: its heading, an explanation as a note or a muted line under it, choices under medium-bold sub-headings, and one primary button at the foot, at the start side, as wide as its words and named for the action. It has no Cancel, because leaving the section cancels, and an action that cannot be undone keeps the danger style and its confirmation (SPC-073) (changes the design standard, decided 2026-11-14).
- **SPC-154** · new · A table in space settings follows NAV-157, puts the title column first and widest and the actions last, as quiet links with words or as icon buttons that carry a name for screen readers, has no stripes, and shows a sort mark on the header of the sorted column. Filters are labelled fields above the table, at the start side, which apply at once without a button, as on every list (ADM-103). A space's name follows a title, smaller and in brackets, only in a report that spans spaces (changes the design standard, decided 2026-11-14).
- **SPC-155** · new · Where one item has several rows in a settings table, such as a page with several restrictions, its rows form a group: the first cell spans them with its content at the top. Where an item holds others, such as a set of templates, a two-level list shows the level by weight alone (changes the design standard, decided 2026-11-14).

## 2.12 The look of a space

A space can look a little different from the rest of the instance, within the design standard.

- **SPC-156** · later · A space follows the look of the instance until a space administrator chooses another look among those the instance offers, and can go back to the look of the instance. A look is a set of named token values that passes contrast in light and dark, as ADM-014 checks, never a style sheet (decision 10), and holds at most an accent colour and a picture for the top of the overview. What else a look may change, and who makes looks, needs its own study.
- **SPC-157** · later · The section Look of the space shows the current look in a small table, with a choice column, a framed preview, the name in bold and a description, and under it the looks to choose from in the same columns, with one button that applies the chosen look and stays disabled until one is chosen. With none to choose, the list says so in one muted sentence.
- **SPC-158** · new · A space administrator writes, in the section Layout, a header and a footer that show above and below every page and blog post of the space. They are written in the editor like a page and may hold text, links, images from the space's files and dynamic blocks; each reader sees them as the editor renders a page (PAG-122), a dynamic block in them shows each reader only what they may see (NAV-006), an image from an outside address is not fetched (decision 5), and empty means none.
- **SPC-159** · new · In Layout, the header and the footer sit under a sub-heading and one line that says where they show, each with a small bold label above an editor field of about three lines, and Save and Reset changes under them (SPC-152). Whether they go into exports and public sites, and whether a change makes a version with an audit entry, needs its own study.
